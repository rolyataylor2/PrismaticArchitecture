# Part Presets

This directory contains reusable definitions for starting Parts. A Part may diverge from its original preset after experiences.

Author names in fewer than six words describing feelings or motivations. Missions describe what the Part believes serves the Agent; strategies describe methods and beliefs describe relational assumptions. General Parts refer to broad subjects such as others; retain a specific referent only for a Part about that relationship. See ADR 0092 and `docs/theory/MISSION_AND_BIAS.md`.
