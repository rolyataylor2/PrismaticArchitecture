# Consolidation Reconsolidation

Offline processing may reopen subjective memories whose meaning is relevant to current concerns and may have become unstable in light of new evidence or context. The owning Part may compare its prior interpretation with evidence and propose an incremental semantic revision, or resist revision.

An accepted revision creates a new version; historical versions remain immutable. Preserve the distinction between objective history and current subjective meaning. Objective evidence may inform a Part but does not automatically overwrite its account. No fixed instability test or revision formula is selected here. Documentation scaffold only.
