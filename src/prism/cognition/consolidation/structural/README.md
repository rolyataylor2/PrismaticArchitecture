# Structural Consolidation

Offline consolidation may semantically propose new Part formation (including relational Parts), generalization, specialization, split, merge, dormancy, reactivation, archival, trigger changes, association changes, or emotional-charge changes. Recurrence and co-activation inform judgment; neither dictates a structural operation. Repeated referent mentions alone do not justify a Part.

Specific relational learning and transferable general learning should remain distinguishable. Generalized learning may persist after its source relationship is no longer active. Provenance and lineage remain inspectable without automatically becoming activation triggers.

All accepted structural changes pass through normal lifecycle and mutation validation, preserve ancestry and history, and produce versioned state. When evidence is ambiguous, preserve existing distinctions. Do not optimize for minimum Part count, smallest memory, or maximum consistency. This is scaffolding only.
