# Consolidation Replay

“Replay” can refer to two distinct activities. **Ordinary consolidation replay** (the default) reactivates or reconsiders psychologically digested material: Part memories, beliefs, predictions and outcomes, thoughts, associations, conflicts, and relational representations. It does not routinely load objective event records. It begins from cognitive traces rather than treating every raw event as equally meaningful.

**Ancestor-event replay** (exceptional) is controlled retrieval of an interpretation-light experience record through an opaque ancestry reference, for re-exposure to its current owning Part. It is rare, explicitly traceable, and not implied by ordinary consolidation replay. Candidate developmental signals may include repeated activation, prediction failures, charge, conflicting later evidence, divergent Part memories, unresolved contradiction, role change, or Identity development. These are experimental possibilities, not fixed rules or schedules.

The current Part interprets any re-exposed experience from its present developmental position. Objective information is evidence; it does not dictate psychological meaning or correct memory. Different Parts' accounts remain distinct. Replay records should make ancestry, reason, before/after interpretations, and accepted or rejected mutations inspectable. This is documentation scaffolding only.
