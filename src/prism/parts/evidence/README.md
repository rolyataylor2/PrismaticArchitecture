# Part Evidence

Parts accumulate lived evidence supporting or challenging their perspectives.

A major source of evidence is the relationship between:

- a Part's proposal;
- its prediction;
- whether Self selected the proposal;
- what later occurred.

Conceptually:

```text
prediction
    ↓
time / condition
    ↓
observed result
    ↓
Part interpretation
    ↓
possible mutation
```

Evidence belongs to the Part's subjective developmental process. It is not a global reward score.

## Suggestions and results

Early The Prism Agent Architecture prototypes referred to this concept as `suggestions_and_results`. The permanent field name is undecided.

The underlying concept is retained: a Part should be able to remember what it advocated, what it expected, and what later happened.
