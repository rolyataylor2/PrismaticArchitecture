# Relational Parts

Relational Parts represent learned relationships between the Agent and something meaningful.

Possible anchors include:

- people;
- objects;
- places;
- concepts;
- roles;
- projects;
- situations;
- internal states;
- other Parts.

A relational Part is not merely a factual database record. It contains the Agent's learned relationship to the anchor. For example, a Paul Part may contain how interaction with Paul tends to feel, what patterns have been observed, what strategies have worked, and what the Agent expects.

Relational Parts may have low or high emotional charge. A low-charge recognition Part may activate without producing a behavioral bid. See [associations](associations/README.md) and [provenance](provenance/README.md).
