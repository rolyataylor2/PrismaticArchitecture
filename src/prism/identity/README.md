# Identity

Identity is the Agent's persistent self-definition: who it understands itself to be and who it wants to become. Its content is a collection of question-and-answer Traits with context-search keywords and creation timestamps (ADR 0099); questions can express values, roles, self-conceptions, aspirations, preferences, commitments or other self-definitions.

Identity = persistent reference. Self = chooser. Self reads Identity while selecting among Part proposals. Identity does not inspect WorldState, receive bids as an active chooser, deliberate, predict, or execute actions. Temporary Identity masks are distinct overlays.
