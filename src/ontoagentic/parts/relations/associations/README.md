# Part Associations

Parts may maintain meaningful associations with other Parts or concepts. Associations can help activation propagate.

Examples:

```text
Paul → friendship
Paul → personal questioning
personal questioning → caution
```

Associations are not authority. One Part does not command another.

## Distinguish associations from other relationships

- **Association:** “These concepts are related.”
- **Trigger:** “This circumstance can make this Part psychologically active.”
- **Provenance:** “This experience contributed to this Part becoming what it is.”
- **Lineage:** “This Part descended from another Part through split, merge, or succession.”

These concepts must not be collapsed. In particular, provenance must not automatically become a trigger.
