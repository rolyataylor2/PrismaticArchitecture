# Prediction Evaluation Support

This runtime support concerns when an owning Part's open prediction becomes evaluable. A Part normally has at most one unresolved prediction. Its expected horizon may be time-based, event-based, or conditional; decisive evidence may permit early evaluation, while expiry without evidence need not mean confirmation or contradiction.

The mechanism may retain enough information to surface relevant later evidence to prediction evaluation. It does not interpret psychological correctness and is not a generic task scheduler, cron system, automation framework, or independent prediction queue.
