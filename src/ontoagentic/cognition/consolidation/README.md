# Cognitive Consolidation

Consolidation is a slower offline developmental process, distinct from online/waking cognition. Waking cognition primarily interprets the present and makes local updates; consolidation asks what psychologically selected experiences collectively taught the Agent. It may be analogous to sleep or rest, without requiring biological sleep, and does not replace the canonical waking loop.

## Preferred input and experience digestion

Start from Part episodic memories and changes, activation and activation-spike traces, co-activation, prediction context/evaluations and repeated confirmations or errors, meaningful thoughts, emotional charge, repeated referents and triggers, associations, conflict, and Identity incongruity. Raw world/audit history is secondary evidence, not a psychologically uniform nightly input. OntoAgentic digests experiences: ordinary cognition forms persistent subjective structures, then sleep primarily processes those already digested traces. **Digest first; re-open rarely.**

## Conceptual pipeline

```text
recent cognitive period
→ psychologically relevant, digested traces
→ recurrence and repeated-pattern analysis
→ ordinary consolidation replay of Part perspectives
→ cross-experience developmental reasoning
→ compare experiences and test structural interpretations
→ propose developmental changes
→ normal mutation/lifecycle validation
→ versioned commits
→ retrieval / association index updates
```

Ordinary replay reconsiders digested psychological material. It does not routinely reload objective experiences. A separate, rare ancestor replay may explicitly retrieve an ancestor event for a current Part to reinterpret; objective history provides evidence, not psychological truth. Reconsolidation does not require ancestor replay. See [ordinary replay](replay/README.md), [ancestor replay](ancestor_replay/README.md), [relational](relational/README.md), [structural](structural/README.md), and [reconsolidation](reconsolidation/README.md) scaffolds.

Structural proposals may include relational formation, generalization, specialization, split/merge, dormancy/reactivation/archive, trigger and association changes, memory reconsolidation, or charge changes. Repeated co-activation and referents are evidence to inspect, not automatic lifecycle rules. Specific knowledge may move toward relational Parts while transferable lessons remain generalized; provenance remains historical and non-activating. Associations are not control links. Charge is distinct from activation Score, stickiness, prediction confidence, and bid authority.

All accepted changes pass through normal validated, versioned mutation and lifecycle mechanisms. When evidence is ambiguous, preserve the existing psychology. Consolidation does not optimize Part count, memory size, consistency, or objective truth. It may strengthen, weaken, create, preserve, or differentiate patterns.
