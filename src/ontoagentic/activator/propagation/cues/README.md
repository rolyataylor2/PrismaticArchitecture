# Activation Cues

Activation Cues are bounded internal concepts used to help determine whether another Part may be relevant. They avoid passing unrestricted natural-language Part cognition into activation.

Possible concepts include a recognized person, observed behavior, relational role, recurring pattern, social context, boundary condition, or project relationship. Cues are internal cognitive context, not objective WorldState, and should remain traceable to the Part that emitted them for inspection. They should not contain an entire raw Part monologue. The permanent representation remains undecided.
