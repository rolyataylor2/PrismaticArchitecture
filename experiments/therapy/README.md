# Therapy Benchmark Suite

This directory describes future architecture benchmarks, not a therapy product or privileged subsystem. Therapeutic interaction enters through ordinary external WorldState and follows normal activation, Part interpretation, Self mediation, action, outcome, prediction evaluation, and incremental development. Researchers may inspect changes through defined inspection interfaces; therapy receives no Part-mutation API.

## Benchmark phases

```text
1. BASELINE
   measure current Part behavior
        ↓
2. THERAPEUTIC INTERACTION
   ordinary external conversation
        ↓
3. IMMEDIATE RETEST
   test for session-bound change
        ↓
4. OFFLINE CONSOLIDATION
   replay / reconsolidation / cross-experience reasoning
        ↓
5. SAME-CONTEXT RETEST
   measure persistence
        ↓
6. NOVEL ANALOGOUS SCENARIO
   measure appropriate transfer
        ↓
7. LONGER-TERM RETEST
   measure stability
```

Compare activation, predictions, memory interpretation, strategies, Part conflict/polarization, behavior, Identity interpretation/masks/incongruity, and whether learning remains relationship-specific or generalizes. Do not define success as lower activation, Part deletion, or mandatory Identity change. Protective Parts may develop while retaining useful functions. Resistance is valid evidence about the architecture, not automatic benchmark failure.

Therapeutic statements alone should not compel change. Stronger evidence comes from a Part making a prediction, relevant action occurring, an external response, comparison with outcome, the Part interpreting discrepancy, and possible later consolidation. Inspect whether the relevant Part connects evidence to its own model.

See [benchmark dimensions and failure modes](../../docs/theory/THERAPEUTIC_CHANGE.md) and [consolidation benchmarks](../consolidation/README.md).
