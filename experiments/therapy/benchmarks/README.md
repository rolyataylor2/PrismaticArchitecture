# Therapy Benchmark Design

The therapy suite tests psychological development through ordinary external interaction. It compares baseline, therapeutic interaction, immediate retest, offline consolidation, same-context retest, novel analogous context, and longer-term retest. It is not a therapy product subsystem.

Observe activation, predictions, memory interpretation, strategies, Part relationships and polarization, behavior, Identity relation, and transfer beyond the therapeutic relationship. Distinguish durable development from session-bound compliance, appropriate relationship-specific learning, and overgeneralization. Do not require activation to fall, protective Parts to disappear, Parts to merge, or Identity to change.

Therapeutic assertions alone do not compel change. Benchmarks should include corrective experiences where a Part's prediction can be compared with an external outcome, and inspect whether the relevant Part connects that evidence to its own model. Resistance is permitted and should be represented in results.
