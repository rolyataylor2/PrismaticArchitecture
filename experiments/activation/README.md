# Activation Experiments

This area is intended to evaluate the quality of Part activation.

## Candidate-set stability

Hold:

- WorldState constant;
- target Part constant;
- target trigger constant.

Vary unrelated candidate Parts. Measure whether the target Part's activation judgment remains stable. Additional context may clarify semantic interpretation, but unrelated candidates should not substantially shift the assessment.

## Vector-filter sensitivity

Vary candidate retrieval breadth. Determine whether psychologically important Parts are lost before Jev evaluation.

## Simultaneous activation

Construct situations in which several contradictory Parts should all be highly active. Verify that the decision model permits several high Scores simultaneously rather than forcing one winner.

## Trigger specificity

Compare broad and specific Parts against the same WorldState. Evaluate whether semantic activation distinguishes direct trigger match, weak contextual relationship, and historical provenance only.

## Recognition versus emotion

Test whether a relational recognition Part can receive high activation while maintaining low emotional charge and no behavioral bid.

These experiments evaluate the activation mechanism. They should not directly alter Part psychology.
