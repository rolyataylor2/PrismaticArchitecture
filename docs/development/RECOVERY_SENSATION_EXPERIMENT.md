# Current recovery versus remembered fatigue

The embodied-simulation thread and preserved Coupler post-meal observation motivate a testable question: does explicit current strength evidence help ordinary cognition distinguish recovery from remembered fatigue? The simulator previously withdrew its weakness warning after actual food-derived energy restored propulsion. The Part acknowledged no hunger but continued to describe leg fatigue. Simulator `b0430f7` now gives new leg sensors a positive sufficient-supply signal. No cognitive policy has been changed in response.

Shared Simulation Tests `recovery-sensation-silent` and `recovery-sensation-explicit` provide a small authored-text comparison. Both create fresh Agents with identical explicit general-values Identity, no seeded disposition, one activation pass per observation and Executive disconnected. They receive identical fatigue and exhaustion experiences, then a current post-meal report with no hunger. Only the treatment's current leg sensation adds “I feel strength in my legs for walking and jumping.” No desired response, task, activation, hypothesis, Part, bid or Self choice is supplied.

Inspect actual thoughts, remembered evidence, predictions and selected behavior. Distinguish current weakness assertions from earlier weakness recollections and retained uncertainty. Missing choices or inference failure do not establish benefit. Review semantic outcomes with cited evidence; bundled mocks establish registry/pipeline behavior only, and default measures remain inconclusive.
