# Cognitive strategy learning and sleep evidence reconciliation

Source: [complete researcher thread](METACOGNITIVE_LEARNING_THREAD_SOURCE.txt). Decision: [ADR 0180](../architecture/decisions/0180-sleep-prediction-evidence-reconciliation.md).

## Source interpretation

The final conclusions supersede the early perceptual-trace subsystem and dedicated metacognitive disposition proposals. Ordinary Parts propose cognitive strategies using existing predictions and temporary relevance. Primitives and current activation responsibilities stay unchanged. The source's historical primitive-plus-JEV sketch does not restore primitives removed by ADR 0133 or replace current soft-association/asynchronous scheduling. No additional activation pass is added.

Historical evidence is recorded subjective experience, not omniscient world history. Reports, counterfactuals and inferred strategies do not become personally observed events. Historical support/challenge stays distinct from prospective validation/invalidation, obsolete context and unverifiable evidence. Review feeds existing consolidation with unchanged durable-usefulness gates.

The source text is preserved with trailing whitespace normalized. The original attachment has SHA-256 `7912c50d9183ed939a1b3852e55d53e7bbfaf88bbb750bfad951c9cd5869a1fe`.
