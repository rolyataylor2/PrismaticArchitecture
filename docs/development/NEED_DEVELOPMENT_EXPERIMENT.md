# Need-tracking dispositions: authored prior versus experienced development

Status: runnable experimental protocol, 2026-10-23. No configured-model or human-psychology result is claimed.

The user asks whether a disposition that tracks hunger's progressive effects would help, and how such a concern could form outside an authored test. The simulator now supports object-resource depletion, resource-dependent propulsion and sensor-owned English weakness reports. Those mechanics establish consequences, not motivation. This Agent-side protocol extends the shared Simulation Tests registry under ADRs 0135/0138/0177 without modifying the cognitive runtime.

## Four conditions

| Registry ID | Starting bodily concern | Intervening strength observations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| need-development-unseeded-consequences | None | Reduced strength alongside hunger |
| need-development-unseeded-stable | None | Stable strength alongside hunger |
| need-development-seeded-consequences | Explicit authored bodily-continuity disposition | Reduced strength alongside hunger |
| need-development-seeded-stable | Same authored disposition | Stable strength alongside hunger |

All four use the identical explicit general-values Identity, avoiding demo dispositions. Seeded versus unseeded definitions have identical experiences. For a given seed condition, only the two strength-report observations differ; hunger cues, caretaker meals, number/order of stages and later probes are identical. The starting disposition expresses concern for bodily comfort and continuity, not an eating policy, action sequence or guaranteed activation. It remains an authored prior and cannot count as a learned concern.

Each fresh isolated Agent receives a comfortable baseline, two hunger/strength/externally supplied nutrition sequences, ordinary offline consolidation, an early-need probe, a comfortable unrelated control, a changed-setting/bread probe, another consolidation and a retained familiar probe. Executive is disconnected and each observation has one ordinary activation pass. Stage timing is an authoring sequence, not calibrated physiological elapsed time. Caretaker meals happen independently of any Agent proposal and cannot establish autonomous feeding.

## Proposed route outside this apparatus

The general developmental hypothesis is: actual bodily sensations and changed ability become ordinary experience; a Part or bounded coverage may propose an unresolved relationship; temporary prediction-bound dispositions can track later discriminating evidence; ordinary activation/recall and prediction reflection may produce attributed episodes and usefulness evidence; conservative offline consolidation may institutionalize a reusable concern after the originating investigation ends. The concern could then advocate obtaining nourishment before impairment recurs, while competing with other Parts through world-blind Self.

This is the existing general-purpose route, not a new hunger detector, reward function, global need manager or automatic motive. A caregiver providing food, voluntary eating, and unrelated strength recovery must remain distinct causal sources. Missing hypotheses, unresolved investigations, absent repeated usefulness or no persistent descendant are meaningful gaps. Two exposures and two sleeps may be insufficient; no structure should be fabricated to make the test pass. Adequate prior knowledge can also yield sensible advocacy without durable learning.

Outside the vignette, the same hypothesis must be tested with genuine part-derived WorldState and independently recorded action consequences through the Coupler. The executive chooses only from a selected bid and action catalog; the Agent remains mechanics-blind. No researcher should inject these authored meals into a live history as if the Agent selected them. Physical resource mechanics, cognitive relevance, chosen intention and actual food consumption require separate evidence.

## Review and limitations

The sidebar/API exposes all four definitions and saved stage evidence. Compare baseline, waking and post-consolidation versions, prediction origins, scaffold eligibility/usefulness, episodes/recall and persistent descendants. Require actual source lineage before attributing development. Inspect proposed and Self-selected advocacy independently; food vocabulary or hunger mention alone is insufficient. Compare all four runs with identical configured models/prompts and declared budgets; inspect consumed computation as well. No single run establishes the seed's benefit or natural formation. Fixed order, shared thread, pretrained food knowledge and exogenous relief limit causal/durability claims. A changed room is a transfer probe, not an independent context-free durability study.

All four psychological measures default to review-only inconclusive. Mocks establish registry, ordinary-pipeline, source-preservation and persistence behavior only. Configured-provider failure is an infrastructure failure, distinct from an unobserved hypothesis. Optional model assessments remain fallible researcher judgments. Adult-human vignettes do not establish physiological calibration, empirical psychology or DSM qualification; machine/foreign-body adaptation requires separate profiles.
