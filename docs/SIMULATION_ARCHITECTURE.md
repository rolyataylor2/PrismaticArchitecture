# OntoAgentic Simulation Architecture

OntoAgentic simulations give cognitive agents experiences without controlling their psychological responses.

## Three layers

**World** is authoritative external reality. **Interface** transforms world information into agent-specific observations and intentions into attempted actions. **Mind** is the OntoAgentic agent that interprets observations, invokes Parts and cognition, selects behavior, learns, and changes.

## Interaction cycle

1. The simulation advances or an event occurs.
2. Perception produces an observation.
3. The agent interprets it and cognition may react.
4. The agent chooses an action.
5. Validation determines what can occur.
6. Accepted changes update authoritative state and produce outcomes.
7. Events are recorded and new observations become available.

Storylets describe situations that may become possible rather than fixed branches. Language models may assist with dialogue, narration, interpretation, or authoring, but structured state and validated transitions remain authoritative.
