# Causal Attribution

Prediction evaluation preserves a Part's interpretation of why an outcome occurred. A mismatch between forecast and outcome does not automatically mean the Part's model or strategy was wrong.

Evaluation keeps distinct:

- the Part's prediction;
- the action Self selected;
- what external execution actually occurred;
- interruption or external interference;
- objective outcome evidence;
- the Part's causal interpretation.

For example, a Part may predict that leaving at 8:00 gets the Agent to school on time. If the Agent leaves then but an unexpected road closure causes lateness, the Part may attribute the outcome to the unusual closure rather than conclude that leaving at 8:00 always fails. Another Part may interpret the same evidence differently.

The developmental sequence remains incremental:

```text
prediction becomes due
    ↓
objective outcome evidence
    ↓
Part considers what occurred
    ↓
Part performs causal attribution
    ↓
Part evaluates its model
    ↓
possible incremental mutation
```

Causal attribution is subjective and developmentally meaningful. Do not collapse it into a global reward score or naive success/failure reinforcement. The exact evidence model and attribution process remain experimental.
