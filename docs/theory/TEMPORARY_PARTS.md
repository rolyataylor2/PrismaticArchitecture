# Temporary Parts

The default constructed model is defined by [ADR 0126](../architecture/decisions/0126-constructed-perspectives-and-dispositions.md). It supersedes the older persistent-Part meaning below for constructed Agents. Subjective memories retain their bias; temporary instances retain working continuity and remain in the inspector after retirement.

Not every Part needs to become a permanent component of personality. Short-lived motivated perspectives may form around immediate emotionally meaningful objectives.

## Example

A persistent Part may hold “Finish college,” creating local objectives such as finishing tonight's assignment, reaching class on time, or preparing for an exam. Temporary Parts can provide focused motivational pressure.

## Persistence through lived evidence

Most local objective Parts should disappear, become dormant, or become irrelevant after resolution. Repeated failure or other lived evidence may cause a temporary Part to broaden: “If I don't constantly push, homework never gets finished” may develop into a broader academic-completion perspective.

## Mission-support relationship

A temporary Part may form because another Part's mission created its importance. This provenance does not require a recursive hierarchy of autonomous Part managers; both remain Parts in one cognitive system, with Identity retaining final deliberative authority.

## Completion and failure

Completion should generally reduce the pressure keeping a temporary Part active. Failure may extend its lifetime, change its strategy, broaden its mission, strengthen beliefs, or contribute to persistence. The mechanism remains experimental.
