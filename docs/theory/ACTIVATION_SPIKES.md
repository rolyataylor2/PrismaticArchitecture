# Activation Spikes

Activation level describes how relevant a Part is now. An **activation spike** describes a psychologically meaningful rapid increase, salient onset, or unexpectedly high emergence in that activation. They are separate observations: a Part can remain highly active with little new change, or become moderately active through a sharp increase.

A significant spike may be supplied to the owning Part alongside the shared immutable WorldState, current Part state, and its own subjective memories. The Part interprets why the change matters; runtime reports the change without assigning its meaning. A spike can invite a prediction or memory-encoding opportunity, but does not require either and does not directly determine behavior.

Keep these dimensions distinct:

- activation level: current relevance;
- activation change/spike: salient change in relevance;
- emotional charge: emotional significance of the Part or relationship;
- stickiness: continued candidate consideration across cycles;
- prediction confidence: the Part's expectation about a prediction.

No fixed threshold, formula, or policy is established. Spike traces may later inform inspection, memory salience, replay selection, formation, or volatility research, without automatically triggering any of those processes.
