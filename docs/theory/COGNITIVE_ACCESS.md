# Cognitive Access

OntoAgentic deliberately limits what different cognitive entities may directly access.

## Part access

A Part has direct access to its own beliefs, mission, subjective memories, learned strategies, and relevant predictions. Parts do not automatically inspect the complete private memory of other Parts.

## Self access

Self accesses information exposed through the current integrative context, such as Identity, relevant Part reports, selected subjective memories supplied through mediation, candidate actions, and uncertainty. It does not receive raw WorldState, current perception, objective ancestor-event history, the complete Agent database, or a canonical autobiographical memory. Self cannot resolve Part disagreement by inspecting ancestor events; it may ask Parts why they remember an experience as they do.

## Audit access

Developer and researcher systems may possess authoritative simulation event history, cognitive transition logs, historical snapshots, and evaluation information unavailable to the Agent. Privileged inspection state is not automatically cognitive memory.

## External records

The Agent may gain factual information through ordinary environmental interaction, such as reading a journal or checking messages. That information becomes another experience rather than invisible omniscience.

Different experiments may expose different information to Identity, Self, and Parts; boundaries should remain configurable where practical.
