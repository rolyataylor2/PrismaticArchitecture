# Identity and Self

Identity is the Agent's persistent self-definition: values, attributes, roles, aspirations, commitments, and the answer to “Who am I?” or “Who do I want to be?” It is not another Part, an outcome optimizer, or an active decision-maker.

Self is the temporary unmotivated selector and mediator. Self reads Identity while selecting among Part proposals. Parts interpret WorldState, carry emotional and mission-biased perspectives, and forecast consequences. Self may question and mediate Parts, but does not directly receive WorldState or independently forecast the external world.

Identity may develop through sustained experience and mediated reflection. A temporary Identity mask may change how Self applies Identity without replacing core Identity.
