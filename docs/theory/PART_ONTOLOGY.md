# Part Ontology

The default constructed model is defined by [ADR 0126](../architecture/decisions/0126-constructed-perspectives-and-dispositions.md). It supersedes the older persistent-Part meaning below for constructed Agents. Subjective memories retain their bias; temporary instances retain working continuity and remain in the inspector after retirement.

A Part is a learned, bounded Agent–world relationship or motivated perspective with developmental continuity. It may be persistent or temporary. It is neither a complete person nor a disposable role prompt. It can organize around a person, animal, relationship, place, project, task, recurring behavior, social pattern, institution, concept, responsibility, another Part, internal state, or class of situations. A Part can recognize and contextualize without advocating behavior; not every Part needs a strong emotional charge, behavioral bid, danger concern, or permanent role. A Part may have subjective memories, a mission, predictions, or strategies according to its perspective.

Every persistent Part should conceptually have a mission, concern, need, purpose, or desired condition. Missions may be broad or narrow, rational or irrational, and may remain useful long after their originating conditions change.

A Part's mission may concern internal cognitive behavior, such as reducing indecision, ensuring reflection, challenging overconfidence, protecting Identity consistency, or noticing repeated cognitive errors. These are ordinary Parts, not a separate meta-agent species.

A Part is biased toward what it cares about. Safety notices danger; novelty notices opportunity; rejection sensitivity may overinterpret ambiguity. Bias supplies differently weighted reports to Identity.

Relational specificity and generalization can coexist: person-specific knowledge belongs with the relevant relationship where it has formed, while transferable patterns may remain on generalized Parts. Historical provenance can explain where a lesson arose without becoming a current trigger.

Emotional charge is a property of a Part or relationship, not a Part type. It may influence persistence, continued cognition, attention, consolidation, computational resources, or urgency, but does not determine truth, control, bid victory, or activation Score. Keep charge distinct from activation Score, prediction confidence, and bid content. Emotional meaning can emerge from mission, beliefs, subjective memory, relationship, expectations, charge, and interpretation; a separate mandatory emotion engine or taxonomy is not required.

A Part has continuity while its properties can change. It can be mistaken, catastrophize, minimize, generalize, become overconfident, rely on outdated evidence, or detect patterns others miss. A Part is not defined by intelligence: identity comes primarily from persistent motivated perspective and history. Possible characteristics include persistence or stickiness across cycles and an ordered strategy for carrying out a multi-step proposal; neither is required of every Part. See [Part Stickiness](PART_STICKINESS.md), [Part Execution Order](PART_EXECUTION_ORDER.md), and [Task Persistence](TASK_PERSISTENCE.md).

## Emotional character

A Part's emotional character emerges from relationships among what it believes, remembers, values, predicts, and pursues through its mission. It does not require a fixed emotion field or manually categorized label.

## Temporary and persistent perspectives

Some Parts may support short-lived local objectives and later complete, become dormant, or cease active participation. Repeated experience may broaden a temporary Part into a more persistent perspective without requiring a rigid Part subtype.

## Describing what drives a Part

A Part's short name expresses its own feeling or motivation rather than only its behavior or an operation. Its mission describes how it believes it serves the Agent's ego-centric interests; strategies describe methods and beliefs describe driving relational assumptions. This framing applies to recognition as well as advocacy, without requiring a strong fear, emotional charge, or bid. See [Mission and Bias](MISSION_AND_BIAS.md) and ADR 0092.
