# Language-First Learning

The Prism Agent Architecture Parts primarily develop through semantic change.

A Part's beliefs, strategies, triggers, expectations, and mission interpretation may evolve by changing the language through which the Part understands reality.

## Why linguistic change matters

A Part exists as a motivated interpretation of experience.

Changes such as:

"Everyone will betray me."

becoming:

"People I depend on eventually betray me."

and later:

"I become suspicious when I depend heavily on someone."

represent substantial development.

No numeric reinforcement score is required for these changes to be meaningful.

## Outcome evidence

Predictions and later outcomes provide evidence.

A Part may use that evidence to:

- become more conditional;
- become more confident;
- narrow its triggers;
- broaden its triggers;
- change strategy;
- reinterpret memory;
- alter its role;
- change cognitive sophistication.

## Structured data

Machine-readable information remains useful for:

- identifiers;
- timestamps;
- prediction due times;
- lifecycle state;
- provenance;
- history;
- indexing.

Machine structure supports cognition.

It should not replace semantic meaning.

## Experimental numeric models

Numeric confidence, intensity, embeddings, and other measurements may be used experimentally.

They must not become the sole representation of psychological development.
