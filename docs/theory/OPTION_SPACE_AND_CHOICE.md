# Option Space and Choice

OntoAgentic separates proposal generation from self-directed choice.

Parts and related cognitive processes generate possible approaches. Self chooses among those approaches using Identity.

## Proposal space

At any moment, cognition may make candidate behaviors available, with motivations, forecasts, uncertainty, subjective evidence, and concerns. The exact representation remains open.

## Identity choice

Self evaluates available proposals against Identity: whether an option represents, supports, or contradicts persistent self-definition, and whether contradiction is acceptable.

## Inquiry

Self may question Parts and request alternatives, compromise approaches, clarification, evidence, or less extreme proposals. This expands the proposal space through cognition; Identity remains a reference rather than an action-planning Part.

## Cognitive limitation

The Agent can fail to generate a good option. OntoAgentic must not automatically inject an ideal solution that the current cognitive system did not discover.

Sometimes every available option violates some aspect of Identity. The framework preserves that possibility.
