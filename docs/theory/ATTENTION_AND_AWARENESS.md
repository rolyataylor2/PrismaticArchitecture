# Attention and Awareness

OntoAgentic distinguishes internal existence from reflective awareness. A Part may exist without being relevant, activate without entering Self awareness, or influence cognition without producing a verbal report.

## Conceptual stages

```text
Part exists → retrieved → activated → influences cognition → available to Self awareness
```

These are distinct concepts, not required implementation states.

Self has limited attention and should not automatically receive the complete state of every Part, memory, prediction, observation, action, or historical fact. Salience may be affected by urgency, uncertainty, disagreement, novelty, Identity relevance, predicted impact, failed predictions, or explicit inquiry; the mechanism remains open.

Parts may communicate urgency, direction, uncertainty, valence, proposal, or confidence without fluent explanation. Self may investigate further. Its experience of internal activity is limited and reconstructive: it receives reports and signals rather than transparent access to every process.
