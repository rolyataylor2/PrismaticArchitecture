# Internal Deliberation

Self may meaningfully interact with Parts before externally consequential choices. It may ask what Parts notice, want, fear, predict, use as evidence, how certain they are, what would change their minds, and what they would consider satisfying. Self consults Identity as its persistent reference throughout.

Parts generate the candidate proposal space. Self may request alternative strategies, compromise approaches, clarification, evidence, less extreme proposals, or responses to other Parts. Self cannot silently invent an unexplained optimal answer. Conflicting reports, including about memory, are expected.

Self may terminate deliberation once it has enough mediated understanding. Not every choice requires deep debate: fast selection and deliberative selection both remain Self authority.
