# Generalization and Specificity

## Specific relationship

Person-specific knowledge belongs with that relationship where development supports it.

```text
My Caution Around Paul: “I am cautious around Paul when he asks many personal questions.”
```

## General lesson

Transferable learned behavior belongs in a generalized Part.

```text
My Fear Of Intrusive Questions: “I become guarded when people ask many personal questions.”
```

## Provenance and retrieval surfaces

A generalized Part may retain historical provenance that some learning arose through Paul. Provenance is evidence about development, not a trigger. The **active retrieval surface** contains information used to decide whether a Part may be relevant now. The **historical/provenance surface** explains how it became what it is and must not automatically be indexed for activation. The permanent representation remains open.

Current behavior can activate a generalized Part when it matches its generalized trigger. Paul being present alone does not automatically activate Cautious merely because Paul contributed to its formation.

For example, Paul may activate his relational Part by being present. If he is currently asking many personal questions, that behavior can separately match Cautious's generalized trigger. The source relationship does not need to be copied into Cautious's active retrieval surface for this to work.

## Adaptive abstraction

A behavior may legitimately become disconnected from its original relationship: experience with Paul may teach sensitivity to personal questioning; Paul may later become irrelevant while that sensitivity remains. This is not necessarily pathology. Generalized behavior may outlive its source, and source recall may occur through explicit introspective inquiry without automatic activation.

## Generalization width

The question is whether the Agent generalized the right feature. Competing hypotheses could be “Paul is dangerous,” “people who ask questions are dangerous,” “strangers asking questions are dangerous,” “persistent personal questioning is dangerous,” “people ignoring boundaries are dangerous,” or “coercive questioning is dangerous.” A Part may initially generalize incorrectly. Prediction-driven development can narrow, broaden, split, condition, or soften its generalization as later evidence arrives.

## Broad subjects in generalized psychology

A generalized Part describes its relation to others, people, relationships, or a relevant class. Avoid “The User” as the default psychological referent: it narrows a transferable concern to an interface role. “Others may not want to be open to me” is a relational belief; “The user is closed” is both narrower and framed as an observation.

A Part specifically concerned with Paul may retain Paul in its current mission, beliefs, and triggers. A generalized Part can preserve private memories of Paul and opaque provenance without making Paul its current retrieval surface. Generalizing descriptions must not erase episodic specificity or invent a broader lesson without evidence.
