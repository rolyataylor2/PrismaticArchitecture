# Consolidation and Sleep

## Two developmental timescales

The Prism Agent Architecture distinguishes **online/waking cognition** from **offline/consolidation cognition**. Online cognition primarily asks, “What does this mean right now?” It interprets the present, activates relevant Parts, proposes behavior, predicts, chooses, acts, observes outcomes, and makes relatively local incremental updates. Offline consolidation can ask, “What have these experiences collectively taught me?” It is the primary place for cross-experience structural reasoning. It does not replace the waking loop and need not correspond literally to biological sleep. Scheduling remains open.

Offline processing may consider recurrence, abstraction, generalization, specialization, relational formation, split/merge, memory reconsolidation, association changes, emotional-charge development, dormancy/archive, and repeated Identity incongruity. It may surface evidence for later Self/Part reflection but does not directly rewrite core Identity.

## Experience digestion

The Prism Agent Architecture should digest experiences. An experience enters through WorldState; relevant Parts interpret it and may form a prediction capsule or another encoding opportunity. Later evidence and Part evaluation may form subjective episodic memories during waking cognition. Relevant Parts may also develop beliefs, triggers, associations, strategies, and relational understanding. These persistent psychological products become the primary material for future cognition. The immutable original remains historically available for provenance, inspection, verification, and rare controlled replay, but it is not a perfect autobiographical memory.

Sleep therefore primarily digests already digested psychology. This second-order process is intentional:

```text
experience → first digestion during lived cognition
→ Part memories / beliefs / predictions
→ second-order digestion during sleep
→ broader psychological organization
```

## Selected traces and two kinds of replay

Consolidation starts from what cognition found psychologically important: Part episodic memories and changes, activation level and spike traces, co-activation, prediction capsules/evaluations and repeated confirmations or errors, meaningful thoughts and mutations, emotional charge, repeated referents and triggers, associations, conflict, and Identity incongruity. Raw world/audit records are not routinely reloaded; they remain available as secondary evidence when needed.

**Psychological replay** is ordinary consolidation material: selected Part memories, predictions, thoughts, conflicts, and relational representations are reactivated or reconsidered. **Objective ancestor replay** is exceptional: a controlled process retrieves an interpretation-light ancestor experience for a current Part to reconsider from its present developmental position. These terms must not be conflated. Ancestry is normally opaque and is not an automatic recall link. No fixed replay schedule or selection rule is established.

## Reconsolidation

A subjective memory may be reconsidered through ordinary new evidence or context without any objective replay. Its owner may reinterpret it and create a new semantic version; earlier versions remain immutable. Rare ancestor replay is one possible source of evidence, not a requirement for reconsolidation. A Part may preserve its prior interpretation, soften or intensify it, add nuance, or reject the evidence. Re-evaluation is not automatic truth correction.

Significant reinterpretation should be capable of preserving psychological continuity with the previous view. The exact wording is generative; the current memory need not use a fixed “I used to think” template. Historical versions remain immutable and are not all supplied during ordinary cognition.

## Structural development

Repeated referents and co-activation can suggest forming a relational Part, but a repeated noun alone does not establish necessity. Consolidation asks whether the referent has repeated psychological significance and whether a new representation would reduce confusion or improve cognition. It distinguishes person-specific knowledge from transferable lessons, preserves provenance, and proposes semantic mutations through normal validation and versioning.

Part formation during sleep should primarily inspect psychologically meaningful digested traces: activated-Part traces, memories, recurring referents, emotional investment, prediction outcomes, and co-activation. It should not routinely mine the entire objective event archive for hypothetical Parts. Specific knowledge may move toward a relational Part while transferable patterns remain with generalized Parts. Provenance and lineage remain inspectable but do not automatically enter active retrieval. Generalized learning can outlive its originating relationship. Associations may inform retrieval or bounded activation cues, not command or authority. Emotional charge remains distinct from Jev activation Score, stickiness, prediction confidence, and bid authority.

Consolidation is not an optimizer for fewer Parts or maximum efficiency. Preserve ambiguity and distinct perspectives when evidence is weak. If uncertain, do not consolidate.

The governing invariant is: **when structural evidence is ambiguous, preserve the existing psychology.** Do not optimize for minimum Part count, compressed memory, maximum consistency, or objective truth. Redundancy, contradiction, and context-specific beliefs may be meaningful. Digest first; re-open rarely.

## Benchmarks

A prediction-centered therapy benchmark should record a Part's initial expectation (for example, “If I admit fear, they'll think I'm pathetic”), ordinary disclosure and response, the Part's evaluation and causal interpretation, any waking episode, immediate development, later sleep consolidation, and predictions in same-context and novel analogous retests. Durable change matters more than conversational compliance.

An old-memory re-evaluation benchmark should establish an emotionally important Part memory, allow later experience and/or therapy to develop the Part, observe conflict with the old interpretation, permit a rare ancestor replay, and evaluate how the current Part interprets the experience. If warranted, create a new memory version and verify that the old version remains, the new content is not copied from the objective record, and the Part may retain disagreement with external interpretations.

A sleep digestion-versus-overreplay benchmark should compare healthy consolidation of digested Part state with a pathological condition that repeatedly replays raw objective history. Measure Part differentiation, factual homogenization, motivated interpretation, subjective continuity, and correction rate.
## Cognitive strategy hypotheses and historical evidence

[ADR 0180](../architecture/decisions/0180-sleep-prediction-evidence-reconciliation.md) extends current-profile sleep with bounded reconciliation of ordinary predictions against recorded subjective memories, including experiences preceding the hypothesis. Historical support/challenge does not establish prospective prediction success. Reports and imagined alternatives do not establish personally observed outcomes. Ordinary Parts may hypothesize attention, recall or reasoning strategies without a separate metacognitive authority or memory subsystem. Structural learning still requires recorded usefulness; no retrospective evidence count alone forces crystallization.
