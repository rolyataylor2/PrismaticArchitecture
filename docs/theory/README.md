# OntoAgentic Cognitive Theory

This directory describes the theoretical model of mind that OntoAgentic is intended to explore.

These documents are not implementation specifications. They describe what a Part is, why Parts form, why Parts are biased, how Parts predict outcomes, how Parts develop, why Parts may have different reasoning capabilities, how Identity relates to Parts, how internal disagreement contributes to balanced behavior, and how perception may be mediated through Parts.

OntoAgentic is experimental. These ideas should be implemented so they can be tested, compared, weakened, replaced, or extended.

See [Activation Spikes](ACTIVATION_SPIKES.md), [Prediction Lifecycle](PREDICTION_LIFECYCLE.md), [Prediction Context](PREDICTION_CONTEXT.md), [Episodic Memory Formation](EPISODIC_MEMORY_FORMATION.md), [Relational Parts](RELATIONAL_PARTS.md), [Activation Networks](ACTIVATION_NETWORKS.md), [Generalization and Specificity](GENERALIZATION_AND_SPECIFICITY.md), [Task Persistence](TASK_PERSISTENCE.md), [Part Stickiness](PART_STICKINESS.md), [Part Execution Order](PART_EXECUTION_ORDER.md), [Causal Attribution](CAUSAL_ATTRIBUTION.md), [Experience Digestion](EXPERIENCE_DIGESTION.md), [Memory Re-evaluation](MEMORY_REEVALUATION.md), [Consolidation and Sleep](CONSOLIDATION_AND_SLEEP.md), and [Therapeutic Change](THERAPEUTIC_CHANGE.md) for current conceptual scaffolding.

## Theory versus architecture

Theory asks what a Part is, why it exists, what Identity is trying to do, and what development means. Architecture asks which subsystem owns Part lifecycle, how simulation sends observations to an Agent, and where history is persisted. Do not confuse these levels.

## Theory versus configuration

Many theoretical concepts should eventually be experimentally configurable: Identity perception, Part reasoning ability, formation sensitivity, deliberation depth, and forecasting horizons. A theoretical possibility is not necessarily a required default.
