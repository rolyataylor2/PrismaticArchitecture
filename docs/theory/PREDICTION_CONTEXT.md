# Prediction Context

A committed prediction acts as a **prospective experience capsule**: it preserves the details its owning Part considered relevant while an event is still present in bounded, decaying recent context. Later evaluation should ordinarily use this Part-selected subjective context rather than reconstructing the event from objective audit history.

Conceptually, context may include the expectation, interpretation, relevant words/tone/behavior/social reactions/setting/entities, activation level and change, uncertainty or confidence, expected horizon, and opaque source references to WorldState or experience records. These are examples, not a permanent schema. The Part selects details; do not copy the complete WorldState into every prediction.

This capsule has three related roles: it records an expectation, guides attention toward future evidence that may confirm or challenge it, and anchors which present details may matter for later encoding. The Part's preserved context remains distinct from objective truth. Opaque ancestry references support provenance and controlled inspection, not automatic recall. Digest first; reopen objective experience rarely.
