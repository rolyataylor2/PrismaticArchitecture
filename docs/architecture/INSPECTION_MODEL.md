# Inspection Model

Inspection observes cognitive and simulation processes without owning them. It should make activation reasons, Part interpretations and forecasts, bids, Self inquiry and mediation, Identity references and masks, selected actions, external handoffs and execution status, outcomes, future prediction evaluations, and Part development inspectable. Source-tagged recent Part outputs and execution events may support continuity in bounded WorldState context; inspection/audit records do not automatically become Agent memory or Self context.

The external timeline remains distinct from the internal cognitive timeline:

```text
WorldState → high-recall vector filter → shared-state Jev Scores → activation policy
→ Parts → bids → Self + Identity → action → outcome
→ later Part prediction evaluation → development
```

Inspection must distinguish activated Parts from Self awareness, fast Self selection from deliberative Self selection, core Identity from a mask, and world facts from subjective interpretation.

Execution inspection should distinguish:

```text
VECTOR CANDIDATES
Paul · Cautious · Boundary Protector · Abandonment

JEV ACTIVATION
Paul                 3.8
Cautious             3.1
Boundary Protector   4.0
Abandonment          0.4

DERIVED RANK
1 Boundary Protector
2 Paul
3 Cautious
4 Abandonment

ACTIVATION POLICY RESULT
activated: Boundary Protector, Paul, Cautious
not activated: Abandonment
```

The values above are illustrative only. Inspection must distinguish vector similarity, Jev activation Score, derived rank, activation-policy decision, activation change/spike, and emotional charge; these are not interchangeable. A Part may have high activation without a new spike or a sharp spike at a moderate level. Spike is also distinct from stickiness and prediction confidence. It should also expose Part model capability/provider and latency, activation level and prior level/change or spike, prediction opportunity and formation outcome, the current open prediction and revisions, its Part-selected context capsule, horizon, confidence/uncertainty, opaque source references, later evidence, execution/interruption facts, Part evaluation and causal attribution, any episodic memory and ancestry, later consolidation effects, Self's bounded candidate distribution including `DELIBERATE`, deliberation rounds, and operational telemetry. Activation Scores are not emotional intensity, bid strength, or prediction confidence. Inspection may see operational information that Self does not receive.

Future inspection should also display current Part state, Part version history, individual mutations, originating experience, subjective memories, memory revisions, prediction/outcome evidence, trigger evolution, strategy evolution, split ancestry, merge ancestry, archive/reactivation events, and current versus earlier wording.

Memory ancestry inspection should eventually expose the objective ancestor event ID, subjective memory ID and current version, prior immutable versions, Part owner, formation relationship, whether the ancestor was ever replayed and why, interpretations before and after replay, accepted/rejected mutation, and provenance. Researchers should be able to determine whether replay changed meaning and whether continuity was preserved. This inspection access does not grant the same objective-history access to Self or ordinary Part cognition.

Consolidation inspection should eventually show the input cognitive period; selected replay experiences; Parts/perspectives involved; repeated co-activation and referents; prediction errors; candidate relational Parts and generalizations; specialization; memory reconsolidation proposals; association and charge changes; split/merge proposals; accepted and rejected mutations with reasons; preserved provenance; and retrieval-surface changes. Researchers should be able to ask why a Part formed, what supported a generalization or narrowed trigger, what evidence was rejected, what remained unresolved, and whether consolidation created a change or merely preserved a waking mutation. Association views distinguish trigger, association, provenance, lineage, memory reference, and stickiness; no graph database is implied.

Therapy benchmark inspection should compare before therapy, after interaction, after consolidation, same-context retest, and novel analogous context. It should expose activation, predictions, memory interpretation, strategies, Part polarization/conflict, behavior, Identity relation, and whether learning generalized or remained relationship-specific. This separates durable development from temporary conversational compliance.

A useful developmental view may show:

```text
BEFORE
Dogs are dangerous. Stay away from all of them.

CHANGE SOURCE
Three later experiences.

AFTER
Unfamiliar dogs can be dangerous when their behavior is difficult to read.
```

This exposes psychological development rather than only numerical telemetry.
