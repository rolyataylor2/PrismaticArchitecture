# Offline Consolidation

```text
RECENT COGNITIVE PERIOD
        ↓
collect psychologically relevant traces
        ↓
identify recurrence
- repeated referents
- repeated co-activation
- repeated predictions
- repeated conflicts
- repeated emotional patterns
        ↓
reconsider psychologically digested Part traces
        ↓
cross-experience developmental reasoning
        ↓
compare experiences and test structural interpretations
        ↓
propose developmental changes
        ↓
normal mutation/lifecycle validation
        ↓
versioned Part/lifecycle changes
        ↓
retrieval / association index updates
```

Consolidation primarily processes already digested psychology: Part memories and changes, activation traces, predictions and outcomes, co-activation, recurrence, charge, surprise, referents, associations, and conflict. Raw world/audit history is not routinely reloaded and is not the primary psychological input. Digest first; re-open rarely.

Ordinary consolidation replay reconsiders these psychological traces. Exceptional ancestor-event replay may explicitly reopen an interpretation-light experience for a current Part to reinterpret from its present developmental position. It must be rare and traceable, and no fixed schedule or selection rule is decided. Reconsolidation may happen through new evidence without objective replay. Objective event access is limited to inspection, experiment harnesses, ancestry verification, and controlled consolidation replay; Self and ordinary Part memory retrieval do not dereference it. Self cannot settle Part disagreement by inspecting ancestor events.

Consolidation is conservative semantic restructuring, not a Part-count optimizer. It separates relational specificity from transferable lessons, preserves provenance and ancestry, and uses normal validated mutations. Repeated co-activation can suggest formation but does not compel it; a repeated noun alone is insufficient. When uncertain, preserve existing distinctions.

Online cognition continues at its immediate timescale; offline consolidation does not replace or globally plan the waking loop. It may strengthen, weaken, create, specialize, preserve, or differentiate patterns. It may surface repeated Identity incongruity for later mediated reflection, but cannot directly rewrite core Identity. Subjective memories may be incrementally reconsolidated in new versions; objective history remains secondary evidence and does not overwrite Part meaning.
