# ADR 0010: Parts Are Mission-Biased

## Decision
Parts maintain motivated perspectives oriented toward missions or concerns and are permitted and expected to be biased toward them.

## Reason
The architecture seeks global balance through specialized partial perspectives rather than requiring every component to be independently balanced.

## Consequences
Parts may interpret identical evidence differently and exaggerate risks or opportunities. Bias should not automatically be normalized away.

## Flexible implementation
Mission representation, strength, adaptation, and internal encoding remain open.
