# ADR 0165: Continuous asynchronous cognition

Status: Accepted implementation target, 2026-10-14. Implementation and verification remain pending.

Source: researcher discussion supplied in `pasted-text-1.txt` on 2026-10-14, and the explicit instruction to replace synchronous cognition rather than retain two modes.

Supersedes the online batch-completion barrier, whole-cycle message serialization and synchronized scope retirement in ADR 0133. Retains ADRs 0136/0138/0139's subjective experience, prediction lineage and independent activation semantics, ADR 0157's world-blind affect/behavior selection, and ADR 0162's external execution boundary.

## Decision

One event-driven runtime separates observation intake, disposition activation, bounded independent thinking, the pool of expressed bids, serialized Self decisions and external execution. New observations need not wait for thinking or selection. A completed Part contributes immediately without waiting for its activation cohort. Completion order never directly selects behavior.

Owning-Part Self answers (including requested recall) and focused bid revisions use the same scope queue as initial reasoning. They do not interrupt an active thought or bypass resource bounds. Admission deferral, coalescing and stale owning-version checks preserve the previously expressed proposal without fabricating an answer. Queued or completed inference against a superseded owning version is audit evidence only, not a replacement bid. Standalone selection functions accept a host-supplied admission boundary; the production runtime always supplies it.

Every activation assessment uses one immutable WorldState and frozen direct association scores. Every reasoning job retains its starting snapshot, selected memory versions, disposition version and continuing Part version. Jobs from different assessments may overlap and use different snapshots. Shared thoughts contain attributed expressed products only; completion publishes a later subjective snapshot, never changes an in-flight snapshot or grants private-state access.

## Admission and pause

Resource limits bound concurrent reasoning, queued work, repeated activation and decision work. At most one reasoning job per Agent/thread/disposition scope may run. A running thought is never interrupted by a newer activation. By default currently processing dispositions are excluded from activation evaluation. An editable setting permits scoring them: newer scores update current activation telemetry, without changing the running job's context, launching duplicate work or granting authority. Score ordering follows observation/assessment provenance, not provider completion order. Exclusion is missing measurement, not a zero.

An editable activation-evaluation limit per externally supplied WorldState can be one, followed by an explicit pause. This means one independent scoring pass over eligible scopes, not one winning disposition or one global worker. Already admitted thoughts may finish and publish; pausing prevents further automatic activation passes. Resume and additional observations have explicit provenance and budget behavior. The limit uses the same asynchronous runtime; no legacy synchronous mode remains. Internal thought-triggered snapshots retain the originating external-observation budget lineage so they cannot evade the pause.

New reasoning requires an unprocessed snapshot/reconsideration reason; stable activation alone cannot continuously resubmit identical work. Repeated internal activation is bounded, with coalescing and inspectable deferral. Rejection of a bid does not resolve its concern or prevent later evidence-driven thought.

## Bids, Self and commitment

Versioned bids retain source job, snapshot, activation assessment, timestamps and Part-authored applicability conditions. Only current eligible versions are selectable. Supersession, withdrawal, expiration, selection and external rejection remain inspectable. Repeated submissions cannot multiply one perspective's voting power. Supporting contributions preserve their owners instead of being synthesized into psychological agreement by runtime code.

The pool is scoped to an Agent/thread, with one current expressed contribution per disposition scope. Exact duplicate external actions may share a selectable representative while each supporting perspective remains attributed. This is structural deduplication, not semantic agreement. New observations make older pending bids require review; the owning Part may retain, replace or withdraw its bid through the ordinary answer capability against a fresh immutable snapshot and its own permitted state. That review is admitted through the same bounded scope queue and cannot interrupt running cognition. Host submission requires the reviewed snapshot still to be current and the exact bid version still selected, in addition to existing operational permission/feasibility checks. Unreviewed or superseded late bids cannot acquire execution authority. Recovery preserves bid history but requires renewed review before selection; it never replays an old selection.

Part-authored `validWhile` clauses describe observable external conditions. The separate bounded `bid_applicability` task sees factual-only observations and those clauses/action descriptions, without private Part state or Self context. Every clause must score above 2 on independent 0–4 support judgments; absent or unknown support blocks submission. This is a conservative admission judgment, not authoritative external truth or a psychological correctness score. Exact current versions, grants, tool validation and physical feasibility remain independently enforced. A provider failure is infrastructure failure, not a judged false condition. The deterministic mock reports unknown support rather than pretending to verify a world.

With Executive integration enabled, a recorded pre-submission rejection can appear in later same-thread factual context as a host report that no action was submitted, with its stated admission reason. This narrow feedback contains no private thought, Self argument or audit bundle and does not fabricate an Execution or environmental outcome. Disconnected cognition excludes it along with other Executive feedback.

Self receives mediated expressed contributions, Identity, selected commitment/affect history and factual execution feedback through defined interfaces. It never receives raw WorldState, private memories or audit history. One Self decision can commit at a time; arrivals during selection request a later decision rather than mutate its frozen choice set. Decision triggers include available proposals, selected-action feedback and bounded reconsideration/inquiry. Self can decline action and request further thought through ordinary mediated inquiry. A speech-opportunity Part may advocate speaking but is not a mandatory or privileged speech gate.

Commitments preserve what Self selected, its constraints and observed status. New proposals may advocate continuation, revision or interruption; Self chooses. Host execution validates current feasibility/authority and only permits supported safe interruption boundaries. Neither a late bid nor a high activation score automatically cancels external work. Affect has explicit persistence and a separate bounded reconsideration trigger rather than changing on every bid arrival. No global psychological task manager is introduced.

Ordinary affect selection runs at most once per external-observation lineage after an actual offer or carried focus reaches Self. Further thoughts in that lineage use the committed lens and retain unconsidered offers for a later external experience. Explicit bounded affect inquiry remains within the selecting Self invocation. The affect clock advances on committed choice in a new observation lineage, not on Part completion, provider failure, internal attention passes or external-adapter completion. A serialized decision publishes its chosen focus and commitment before waiting for external execution or later coverage. A later infrastructure failure preserves that committed choice while remaining distinct from execution success. Completed-observation affect summaries remain historical inspection records; the live committed ledger owns carryover.

The affect inspector presents latest committed choices independently of its historical display range, with source-observation status and source links. Historical period cohorts remain explicitly completed-observation summaries. A failed or running source observation does not retroactively make an already committed choice an uncommitted attempt; missing legacy ledger data stays unavailable.

## State, learning and routing

Admission rechecks busy state after activation scoring, so overlapping assessments cannot admit a second generation for a running scope. Commits validate captured versions; late completion never overwrites newer psychology. Invalid/stale commits and provider failures remain explicit infrastructure outcomes. Scope inactivity cannot retire an instance while it owns running thought or selectable contributions. Ended prediction-bound investigations stop fresh admission while preserving completed experiences and lineage.

Predictions preserve formation and completion times and cannot claim prior observation as a successful future forecast. Later factual evidence may evaluate a testable prediction even if its bid was not selected. One unresolved prediction per Part, versioned subjective memory and conservative offline institutionalization remain enforced. Predictions and reflections have serialized version checks independent of behavioral selection. Queued contexts coalesce only within the same scope and inference purpose; an answer or bid review cannot supersede prediction reflection. All purposes share the same noninterruptible running-scope exclusion and bounded queue. A queued reflection captures the current owning version when its job starts, including a retired owner; retirement does not erase a genuinely testable prediction. It never reactivates the owner, and later changes during reflection prevent stale commits.

Prediction evaluation runs on a separately serialized learning lane after thought publication. Available expressed bids can reach Self while outcome judgment or owning reflection remains in flight. Source completion and experience attachment drain admitted learning work, but the Self decision lock is released before waiting for that drain. A learning failure remains inspectable independently of any choice already committed.

Outcome judgment/reflection receives a factual evidence projection containing only observations later than the prediction's committed observation frontier. Earlier selected context remains in the preserved capsule and permitted owning memories, not a second factual channel that could confirm a late-completing prediction. This projection does not mutate the frozen observation or change the full snapshot delivered to ordinary activated Parts.

Reasoning-resource routing is based on a thought's declared difficulty/uncertainty and urgency, not permanent disposition intelligence. Urgency alone does not mean cheap inference. Optional intuitive-to-deliberative escalation preserves scope continuity and proposal revisions; resource/model provenance, latency, prediction outcomes and selection/execution outcomes remain separately inspectable. Provider limitations are not psychological traits. Routing introduces no new motivation or selection authority.

Opt-in routing uses an explicit `thought_resources` workload assessment with only the same immutable owning-scope input allowed for Part generation. It declares simple/complex difficulty, low/high uncertainty and routine/urgent timing, with a reason; these are operational estimates, never persistent Part traits or activation scores. Complex or uncertain work uses the configured deliberative model, including when urgent. Other work uses the intuitive model. Models must share the configured provider credential boundary; routing never sends one provider's key to another. Disabled routing adds no assessment calls. Provider operations record the actual invoked adapter/model rather than the Agent's primary-model setting. Assessment failures remain infrastructure failures. This assessment does not publish a thought, create a prediction or grant action authority.

Committed expressed thoughts may retain an opaque inference-operation/provider reference for researcher attribution. It is host provenance only: exclude it from shared thought content, Part memories and Self. Prediction attribution follows the exact originating thought reference; selections and actual external reports retain their independent bid/contract lineage. Missing provenance stays unknown, never inferred from current Agent settings or textual similarity.

Optional history escalation uses a configurable consecutive-incorrect-prediction threshold (default two). Only bounded outcome checks for predictions explicitly linked to the same scope's thoughts and the actual intuitive provider count. A correct check breaks the consecutive pattern; incomplete/irrelevant checks do not grade model quality. Crossing the threshold selects deliberative resources for a later thought, without rewriting the scope or passing audit records to cognition. This is a replaceable operational heuristic under experiment, not a causal claim that a model caused contradiction. Selection frequency and blocked actions do not count as prediction failures.

Owning-Part answers and focused revisions can independently reassess their own workload under opt-in routing. The assessment receives that scope's immutable experience, private state and the explicit inquiry; Self stays world-blind. An intuitive initial proposal can therefore receive deliberative reconsideration, retaining its owner and revision lineage. Actual revision-model provenance attaches to the revised bid separately from the initial thought's model. Retained proposals keep their original generation attribution; asking a question does not itself transfer authorship or action authority.

## Inspection and experiments

Persist activation assessment history including snapshot/version, assessment order, timestamps, direct/final independent 0–4 scores, exclusion/defer reasons and job status. Each disposition has a historical activation line chart, exact-value evidence rows and drill-down to source assessments. Missing measurements remain gaps; no synthetic zero or emotional-intensity interpretation. Timeline inspection distinguishes observation, inference start/completion, publication, Self selection and execution.

Register ordinary-pipeline Simulation Tests for slow thought overlapping new observations; busy-scope exclusion versus rescore; stale bids; repeated internal activation and bounded attention; serialized decisions and commitment change; unselected prediction learning; and resource-routing latency/quality. Preserve source, initial conditions, controls, criteria, inference budgets, failures and limitations. The one-pass pause configuration supplies a controlled comparison within the sole runtime. Fixture timing tests establish concurrency plumbing, not psychological benefit. Configured-model observations remain separate.

Simulation adapters declare whether time is wall-clock coupled or externally stepped. Inference latency must not silently advance or freeze world time. Record both time domains and compare equivalent budgets; do not claim human psychological validity from simulation results.

Attention-window comparisons use the same wall-clock allowance and concurrent/queued resource ceilings, with one-pass, bounded-pass and unrestricted-pass treatments. The observer starts its timer at the first frozen observation, pauses further admission at the deadline and drains accepted cognition without interruption. Preserve admission/deadline/drain timestamps, all internal source cycles and actual inference/usage counts. Equal ceilings are not proof of equal consumed computation; mismatched or unmeasured work remains an explicit comparison limit. Interrupted windows require a new trial, rather than resuming a historical deadline and calling it uninterrupted latency evidence.

Concurrent experiment observations carry stable host receipt IDs. Explicit recovery reuses already delivered observations, delivers only missing inputs and, after failed cognition, resumes ordinary attention against the current immutable experience rather than replaying earlier world versions. Preserve failed/completed source cycles and recovery cycles together. A recovered stage is not an uninterrupted latency trial; its recovery note must remain visible in evidence.

## Completion obligations

Cut over all public entrypoints and callers; update runtime settings, persisted defaults/migrations, editable factory prompts/task descriptions and lifecycle integration. Verify overlapping intake, independent publication, serialized decisions, pause/resume, busy handling, version races, prediction lineage, execution authority and shutdown/recovery. Inspect rendered settings, live thought evidence and disposition charts on desktop/mobile, including empty/missing history. Documentation alone does not establish implementation.

Shutdown rejects new public runtime intake, pauses automatic attention and drains admitted direct observations/checkpoints and their queued scope work before closing inference admission. Application shutdown first drains operations already accepted by its coordination boundary, including calls not yet entered into the runtime. Shutdown does not cancel a thought, replay a selection or silently discard accepted input.
