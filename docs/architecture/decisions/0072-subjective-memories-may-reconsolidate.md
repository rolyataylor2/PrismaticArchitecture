# ADR 0072: Subjective Memories May Reconsolidate

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Parts own subjective memories that may preserve relational interpretations distinct from objective history. Development needs a way to revise current meaning without erasing continuity or granting audit records authority over subjective experience.

## Decision

When a subjective memory becomes relevant alongside new evidence or context, its owning Part may incrementally reinterpret it. An accepted change creates a new version; prior versions remain immutable. A Part may resist or reject revision. Objective history may inform the process but does not automatically overwrite subjective memory.

## Consequences

Reconsolidation preserves perspective, provenance, and version history. It is distinct from correcting objective records. Evidence selection, relevance, instability, and semantic revision remain open; no fixed formula is established.

## Related decisions

ADR 0016 assigns subjective memory to Parts; ADR 0044 preserves relational reality; ADR 0017 establishes incremental Part change; ADR 0045 requires explicit versioned mutations.
