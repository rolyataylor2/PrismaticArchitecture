# ADR 0002: Parts Do Not Have External Authority

## Decision

Individual Parts do not directly perform externally consequential actions. The complete Agent retains executive authority.

## Reason

Parts represent partial perspectives. Allowing individual perspectives to independently act would turn the architecture into a collection of autonomous agents rather than one mind composed of interacting perspectives.

## Consequences

Parts may advocate, object, predict, request, or bid. External action occurs through the complete Agent's executive process.

## Flexible implementation

The exact executive and arbitration mechanism remains open.
