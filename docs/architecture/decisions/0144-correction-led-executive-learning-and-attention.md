# ADR 0144: Correction-led Executive learning and cognitive attention

Status: Accepted implementation target; requirement-level completion remains unproven.
Date: 2026-10-08
Source: Researcher methodology update and skill-inspection request, 2026-10-08, extending the [Executive thread](https://chatgpt.com/share/6ac7d5eb-8150-83e8-b6cc-d4727cea3e96).
Extends ADRs 0133/0136/0138/0139/0140/0142/0143. Historical decisions remain preserved.

## Continuous execution and bounded attention

An already selected external activity may continue under its immutable execution contract without full cognition for each tool action. Lightweight event-driven monitoring evaluates new immutable observations at meaningful checkpoints: important results, environmental changes, repeated difficulty, before consequential actions and configured idle intervals. Independent disposition activation and frozen coactivation signals estimate cognitive relevance. Novelty/uncertainty registration may separately expose missing coverage; low disposition coverage is never competence or permission to guess. Restoring monitor registration does not restore generative primitive voices, bids, psychological learning or privileged teacher selection. The existing ordinary Part pipeline remains authoritative when cognition runs.

Background attention performs bounded monitoring. Interested attention may recruit normally eligible Parts through ordinary activation, recall and independent ordered cognition against the same immutable snapshot. Thoughts, observations and predictions without bids do not interrupt execution. A bid invites world-blind Self consideration; Self may continue the current activity. Only an explicitly selected intervention changes, pauses or replaces its authorized execution. An independently necessary hard stop does not await activation or Self reconsideration: permissions, prohibitions, captured budgets, unresolved consequential submissions and required risk approvals remain framework-owned gates.

Executive competence and cognitive activation remain independent. Low competence may request ordinary cognition even without an active disposition. High activation permits considering an intervention even when operational competence is high. Neither numerical signal confers bid authority, emotional intensity, psychological truth or permission. Hysteresis/cooldowns suppress repeated unchanged advisory attention; new or materially changed observations can override them, and hard gates never become suppressible. Monitoring is inference scheduling for existing selected activity, not a global goal/task planner or autonomous decision-maker.

Self receives only expressed proposals, mediated observations and the publicly expressed current selected intention/constraints needed to compare continuation with intervention. It receives neither raw WorldState, raw tool payloads, private Part state nor the complete audit contract/history. Refreshing the environment creates a new snapshot; it cannot mutate a cycle already being considered. Concurrent external work and cognition require explicit checkpoint/selection ordering and once-only intervention consumption; restart never replays submitted effects.

## Learning from bid chains

Record the initial selected bid, every selected correction, expressed thoughts/reasons, activation definitions/versions and judgments, the triggering observations, exact contract changes, actual operations and later verified/unverified/failed outcomes. Preserve selected proposals that did not produce immediate external action and failed corrections. Provenance is inspectable audit evidence, not unrestricted cognitive recall or a global reward score.

Procedural consolidation may propose conditional reusable methods from these records. It must infer and expose applicability conditions and the tool operations that enact a branch, rather than merely replaying bids chronologically. Every branch retains its source bids and evidence. Explicit `teachProcedure` remains supported, but is not the sole final route: selected corrective experience can supply a candidate subject to configured consolidation/learning policy. The Executive cannot unilaterally rewrite its behavior after a failure or confer its own authority.

Distinguish candidate, tested, established and deprecated maturity from permission to apply a method. One independently verified episode may justify a candidate; repeated uses preserve support, failures, unknown applicability and escalation evidence. A task succeeding after a correction is temporal evidence, not proof that the correction caused success. Record before/after progress, constraint compliance, recurrence and relevant control/comparison opportunities; keep unsupported causal claims inconclusive. Reconciliation/versioning preserves failed and deprecated methods, variant lineage, prior meanings and rollback history.

Procedural knowledge states how an operation can be performed. Situational preferences and current authorization remain late-bound to the current selected contract. An old archiving preference cannot silently become a permanent requirement or authorize deletion under a new preference. Conflicts request a new cognitive decision. Cognitive concern may request review before a branch without rewriting the underlying skill. Conditional transfer, confidence and autonomous application require separately inspected evidence and current permission; stored success never relaxes hard limits.

## Configuration and inspection

Keep cognitive attention, intervention, competence, novelty/coverage, checkpoint policy, consolidation mode, application confidence, preference revalidation and intervention cooldown settings separate from authorization. Bounds and signal semantics must be explicit; illustrative defaults are experimental engineering choices, not calibrated psychology. Controls may claim runtime effects only when those effects exist.

The Skills & Learning inspector presents current knowledge, variations and a chronological development trail. It links each version to its actual source bid(s), expressed thoughts/reasons, activation context, selected contract changes, execution/validation evidence, and reason for revision/restoration/deprecation. Show recorded variant relationships separately from superficial resemblance. Distinguish missing historical evidence, actual failures, incomplete verification and unsupported causal attribution. Use readable descriptions and comparisons; raw identifiers/JSON belong in optional evidence detail. This is privileged researcher inspection and cannot become direct Self/audit access.

## Verification and status

Sourced Simulation Tests must compare ordinary cognition and monitored activity, high relevance without a bid, Self choosing continuation, independent skill gaps/novelty, selected corrections, unsuccessful corrections, repeated conditional use, changed preferences, authority stops, cooldown/hysteresis and restart. Preserve actual bid chains, contexts, provider provenance, controls and inconclusive observations. Fixtures prove plumbing only; configured-model cognition and conditional transfer require separate retained evidence. No single run proves human psychology.

Existing exact-procedure teaching/use and no-submission guidance are compatibility foundations. They do not complete continuous monitoring/intervention, correction-derived conditional consolidation, maturity, late-bound preference application or the expanded settings. Implementation and verification are tracked in the Executive audit.
