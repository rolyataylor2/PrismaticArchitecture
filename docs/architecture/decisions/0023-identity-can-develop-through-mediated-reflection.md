# ADR 0023: Identity Can Develop Through Mediated Reflection

## Decision

Identity is persistent but capable of changing through introspection and experience.

## Reason

Agents should be shaped by lived experience rather than have permanent self-definitions fixed entirely at initialization.

## Consequences

Environmental events do not directly mutate Identity. Change occurs through cognitive mediation and preserves developmental history.

## Flexible implementation

Revision criteria, rate, representation, and deliberation mechanisms remain experimental.
