# ADR 0013: Part Cognitive Capability Is Heterogeneous

## Decision
Different Parts may possess different levels and forms of cognitive sophistication.

## Reason
Parts may emerge from different developmental circumstances and serve different functions. Identical reasoning would erase meaningful diversity.

## Consequences
Parts may differ in abstraction, language, forecasting, planning, emotional processing, social reasoning, and metacognition. Capabilities may develop over time.

## Flexible implementation
No permanent cognitive-level scale or resource mapping has been selected.
