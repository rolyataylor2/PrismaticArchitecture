# ADR 0132: Direct WorldState disposition activation

Status: Accepted, 2026-10-03. Requested by the researcher.

## Decision

Remove generative perception from online cognition, superseding the broad/detail perception stages and perceptually discovered entity bindings in ADRs 0126 and 0129. The immutable WorldState and thread steering go directly to independent Jev disposition activation Scores, followed by disposition-specific chronological subjective memory accessibility and ordered construction, evidence and cognition (ADR 0131). No perception generation or entity extraction call precedes or follows scoring.

Roots are eligible immediately; children require their prerequisites in the preceding completed thread cycle. Eligibility stays frozen during scoring. Jev evaluates each disposition's scope, cues and concern directly against supplied WorldState, including absence, reclassification, uncertainty and testimony. A parent never grants activation. Scope relevance belongs in each independent score, not a keyword filter or fabricated observation.

Each active disposition has one recall/construction group. Existing binding records retain the entityId field for storage compatibility; new records use a deterministic scope key, not a claim that runtime discovered an individual entity. Broad class scopes are disposition-level judgments, not per-individual activation measurements. Specific relational targets must be expressed in the disposition scope and cues; evidence concerning one target must not transfer to another. Prior-cycle parent activation only establishes eligibility, and the current child's scope must still register independently. No new entity-recognition subsystem is introduced.

Perception schemas, provider support and historical prompt overrides remain readable, but the perception task is absent from the active Settings → Prompts catalog and is never invoked by the online cycle. Current inference inputs omit perception. Self stays world-blind and Identity-guided; execution remains external. Steering is not a planner. Offline learning and retired-instance retention remain unchanged.
