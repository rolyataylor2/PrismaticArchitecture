# ADR 0077: Objective Ancestor Replay Is Rare

## Status

Accepted

## Context

An old experience may occasionally be developmentally relevant to its current Part, but routine replay risks flattening subjective memory and making audit history psychologically authoritative.

## Decision

Offline consolidation may exceptionally reopen an objective ancestor experience for developmental re-evaluation. Ordinary consolidation reconsiders psychologically digested material and does not replay all objective experiences. Ancestor replay must be explicit, selective, and traceable; its triggers and selection criteria remain experimental.

## Consequences

Ancestor replay is distinct from ordinary psychological replay and is not a fixed sleep schedule, automatic recall behavior, or general-purpose objective-memory API. Reconsolidation does not require objective replay.

## Related decisions

ADRs 0062 and 0071 define consolidation inputs and timescales; ADR 0072 allows subjective reconsolidation.
