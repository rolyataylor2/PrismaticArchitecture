# ADR 0076: Experience Is Psychologically Digested

## Status

Accepted

## Context

If future cognition repeatedly reconstructs meaning from raw objective event history, subjective Part memory becomes secondary and the event archive becomes the real autobiographical system.

## Decision

Relevant Parts digest experience into persistent psychological products such as subjective memories, beliefs, predictions, triggers, strategies, associations, and relationships. Future cognition and ordinary consolidation primarily operate on these products. The rule is **digest first; re-open rarely**.

## Consequences

Objective history remains available for provenance, inspection, verification, and rare controlled replay but is not a routine cognition or sleep input. Sleep primarily digests lived psychological traces, including through second-order cross-experience reasoning.

## Related decisions

ADRs 0062 and 0071 define lived-trace consolidation and distinct developmental timescales; ADR 0074 defines the objective-history boundary.
