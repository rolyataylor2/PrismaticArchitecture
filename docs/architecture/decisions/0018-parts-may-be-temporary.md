# ADR 0018: Parts May Be Temporary

## Decision

Parts do not need to become permanent cognitive structures. Short-lived Parts may form around emotionally meaningful local objectives.

## Reason

Temporary specialization allows focused motivation without permanently accumulating every task or concern into personality.

## Consequences

Parts may naturally disappear from active cognition when their mission completes. Repeated reinforcement may cause a temporary Part to broaden and become persistent.

## Flexible implementation

No permanent persistence threshold or lifetime mechanism is defined.
