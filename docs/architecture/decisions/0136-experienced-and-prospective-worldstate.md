# ADR 0136: Experienced WorldState and affective perspective traversal

Status: Accepted, 2026-10-04. Researcher request: https://chatgpt.com/share/6ac32932-dc9c-83e8-ac57-5cfd66a3c862

Supersedes ADR 0133's removal of affective posture carryover, while retaining its code-owned scope assembly, independent parallel ordered Part generation, prior-cycle prerequisites, world-blind Self, external execution and separate offline consolidation. It does not restore steering or a working-state inference task.

## Experienced state

Researcher clarification: prediction evidence means lived experience: accessible subjective memories, previous observations as experienced, dispositions and the Part's interpretation. Forecast formation must not be forced toward an objective worldview. A mistaken or biased future is valid psychological content. External records are available experiences, not privileged access to simulation truth. Later experience may challenge an expectation; neither bounded classification nor runtime code decides its psychological meaning.

Code assembles one immutable experienced WorldState per cycle. External facts remain a distinct factual layer. Attributed thoughts, expressed remembered interpretations, open prospective predictions and the last Self-selected interpretive/affective posture are explicitly subjective layers. All concurrent Parts receive the exact same snapshot. Current retrieved memories remain scoped inference inputs; the shared layer contains only previously expressed memory interpretations, not private memories, beliefs or Part snapshots.

An open prediction contributes its exact expectation, uncertainty/confidence, horizon, selected capsule, conditions and version/owner provenance. Contradictory predictions remain alternatives. Trajectory forecasts appear in the baseline prospective layer; action-conditioned forecasts remain separate branches and never imply their proposed action occurred. Legacy forecasts without explicit kind are unclassified alternatives, never silently baseline facts. Predictions from other threads or historical nonconstructed Parts are excluded.

Predicted futures can activate ordinary dispositions and affect recall now. Activation remains an independent relevance judgment, not truth or authority. New forecasts enter the next immutable cycle snapshot; no recursive same-cycle activation/generation cascade runs. Prediction checks and outcome reflection receive a factual-only WorldState and later external evidence. Subjective anticipation, remembered interpretations and affect never establish confirmation.

## Affect and choice

Parts express rich natural-language affect, interpretation and anticipated experience. Self receives only the current active Parts’ expressed products with Identity and mediated chosen stance history, not raw WorldState, synthesized prospective state, a separate collection of open forecasts, retrieved memories, private Part state or audit history. An inactive/retired Part’s forecast can shape activation but cannot bypass active cognition to speak directly to Self. Affect is not a required label, scalar vector, multiplier or eligibility filter. Dissenting Parts remain ordinary participants.

Self selection adopts the selected proposal's expressed interpretation and affect as a subsequent cognitive posture. Code preserves each selected contribution separately rather than inventing an emotional synthesis. A generatively authored proposal may specify its expected resulting posture; otherwise its expressed current stance is retained with that distinction. Selection confers neither factual truth nor psychological consensus.

Each completed selection records the prior posture, selected interpretations and resulting posture with cycle/thread provenance. Subsequent cognition receives that selected experienced lens. Self may select an internal navigation proposal to revisit an earlier expressed posture with present evidence. Navigation changes the lens for the next cycle, never external facts, memory versions, predictions, developmental history or archived Part lifecycle. Available navigation states are explicit mediated records, not arbitrary database access. Internal navigation is exclusive, bounded to one selection per cycle and never sent to the external executor.

## Further researcher clarification

Imagination is preserved as lived cognition. Factory Part prompts invite the perspective to experience the projected future as its assumed inevitable unfolding; competing Parts may inhabit incompatible assumed futures. This is subjective certainty, not external truth. The prompts express how much anticipation shapes current cognition without runtime numeric weighting or enforced skepticism. Self remains blind to the synthesized future and chooses through active Parts’ thoughts, affect, expectations, bids and answers. It can make objectively bad choices; neither runtime nor provider checks require external optimality. Later experienced prediction error may change Parts’ memories and interpretations, changing what Self can subsequently consider.

Self may request missing interpretations through bounded deliberation. Recruitment and recall must go through normal cognition and preserve the same cycle’s immutable snapshot and prior-cycle disposition prerequisites; Self gains no direct truth access. If no Part supplies information, Self genuinely does not know it.

## Integration and experiments

Existing task schemas and affected factory prompts describe these inputs and boundaries; no separate emotion agent, planner, working-state model or sovereign chooser is added. History and inspection expose provenance and distinguish facts, predictions and stances. Simulation Tests exercise anticipation, factual contradiction/calibration, affect-dependent interpretation and stance traversal through ordinary cognition on isolated Agents, preserving controls, provenance and inconclusive results. Mock runs validate plumbing only.

Semantic calibration and psychological validity remain empirical questions. The runtime must not calculate invented domain accuracy, mandate optimism, flatten disagreement or decide what an experience means.

Deliberation recruitment uses the existing round budget: after active Parts answer, the attributed inquiry independently rescores eligible nonparticipating scopes and recalls memories. New scoped Parts use normal ordered generation against the identical frozen WorldState. Existing participants remain available; prior-cycle prerequisites cannot be bypassed and forecasts formed this cycle cannot create same-cycle attention. New expressed contributions enter the same world-blind selector. Model failure remains infrastructure failure, never a missing-perspective conclusion.
