# ADR 0019: Self Mediates Without Forcing Revision

## Decision

Self may facilitate learning between Parts during integration, while Identity remains the persistent self-definition. Neither directly overwrites Part beliefs or subjective memories.

## Reason

Parts are persistent motivated perspectives. Development should occur because experience becomes convincing to that perspective rather than because a central controller rewrites it.

## Consequences

Self may ask questions, share another Part's view, consult external evidence, choose experiments, and present outcomes. A Part may accept or reject the resulting evidence.

## Flexible implementation

Internal dialogue and reconciliation mechanisms remain experimental.
