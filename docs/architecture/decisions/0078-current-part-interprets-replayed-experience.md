# ADR 0078: The Current Part Interprets a Replayed Experience

## Status

Accepted

## Context

A Part may develop substantially after forming a memory. Reopening an ancestor can let that same continuing perspective reconsider its meaning from a new developmental position.

## Decision

When an ancestor experience is replayed, the currently developed owning Part supplies its present psychological interpretation. Objective history provides evidence but does not provide a corrected subjective meaning or compel an update.

## Consequences

The Part may preserve, soften, intensify, qualify, or otherwise reinterpret its account, or reject the proposed change. Divergent Part perspectives remain valid. Re-evaluation is developmental reinterpretation, not factual correction.

## Related decisions

ADRs 0044, 0072, and 0074 preserve relational subjectivity, incremental reconsolidation, and the objective-memory boundary.
