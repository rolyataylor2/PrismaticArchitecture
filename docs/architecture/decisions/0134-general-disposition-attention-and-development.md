# ADR 0134: General disposition attention and development

Status: Accepted, 2026-10-04. Implements the researcher's [disposition discussion](https://chatgpt.com/share/6ac31e3b-ef5c-83e8-bef9-935849391ba9).

## Meaning and boundaries

A disposition is a general attention and recall sensitivity. Its scope may be an object, person, relationship, situation, activity, method, strategy, temporal constraint or configuration. These remain the same domain type. Multiple dispositions can attend to overlapping observations and retrieve the same subjective episode; they need not agree. Transferable procedural memories need not name the present object.

ADR 0133 remains the online architecture. Each active scope has its own independent Part generation, and continuing instances retain their local unresolved concern. Current observations determine which approaches are relevant, blocked, complete or obsolete. A Part reasons at its disposition's abstraction level and may express emerging relevance in its attributed thought or prediction attention. It does not activate descendants, execute a stored script or become a global planner. Subsequent cycles reconsider relevance; no automatic parent activation or guaranteed selection is introduced.

The discussion explores direct semantic recognition, component convergence, memory bridging, prediction completion and learned abstraction. Expressed thoughts and scoped prediction attention already support later-cycle routes. The BBQ question explicitly requested theoretical exploration rather than an architecture change. Its proposed bidirectional soft network is therefore not a replacement for ADR 0133's explicit prior-cycle hard prerequisites. Soft expected relationships can be expressed in cues; prerequisites remain code-enforced eligibility conditions. Models must distinguish anticipated structure from observed evidence.

## Development

Offline disposition learning considers selected subjective episodes and resolved predictions together with selected chronological activation and expressed cognition traces. These traces provide evidence of coactivation, recurring trajectories, missing abstractions, prediction errors and proposed approaches; they do not provide raw WorldState, private Part snapshots or objective audit replay. Cycle/thread attribution preserves order without joining unrelated conversations into one trajectory.

Learning may create the smallest useful sensitivity or version an existing one. Recurrence is evidence, not an automatic formation threshold. A salient episode may also warrant learning. Prefer enriching existing sufficient structure over duplicates. A useful abstraction improves future attention, recall, prediction or navigation while preserving meaningful exceptions, contradictions and lower-level dispositions. Compression is an optional benefit, never a global count or consistency objective. Tentative knowledge is expressed linguistically in concern/cues and evidence rather than a new authority score.

Learn conditional relevance rather than rigid execution sequences. Refinement may broaden, narrow or qualify cues and dependencies as experience warrants. Failed predictions invite interpretation of what was missing; they do not mandate punishment or a new disposition. Imagined predictions and coactivation alone cannot justify durable learning: supplied subjective memory evidence remains required. Existing evidence validation, versioning, conservative no-change and historical retention remain authoritative. No skill learner, task manager, privileged decision-maker or automatic simulation mutation is added.

Factory activation, memory-accessibility, Part-cognition and disposition-learning prompts and their Settings descriptions must reflect these meanings and permitted inputs.
