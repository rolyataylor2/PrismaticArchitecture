# ADR 0154: Offline inheritance of expressed concerns

Status: Accepted, 2026-10-09. Source: researcher conversation requesting inheritance from prediction-origin thoughts, with temporary scaffolds remaining relevance-only.

Extends ADR 0138. A prediction does not imply a desire, and confirmation of an unwanted outcome does not create motivation. Temporary prediction scaffolds retain their relevance-only meaning and lifecycle.

## Decision

Part-origin prediction creation records the exact expressed thought event, Part version and originating disposition versions. Existing predictions remain readable without fabricated origin metadata. Offline consolidation receives bounded, chronological, attributed trajectories: originating cognition, later cognition supplied that prediction, later expressed cognition of originating disposition scopes (including new Part instances), and ordinary cognition of its scaffold scopes. These associations remain distinguished. Association is not proof that a thought concerns a prediction. Prediction versions and subjective outcome episodes remain distinct evidence. Gap-origin predictions have no fabricated originating Part or desire.

Only INSTITUTIONALIZE may propose a new inherited concern. The proposal states the concern, meaning, cited thought events, continuity/change rationale and why a separate descendant is useful rather than retaining the concern in an existing disposition. Runtime validates same-Agent recorded citations against supplied trajectories and source investigations, requires an originating thought citation and subsequent thought evidence, and retains ADR 0138 ended-investigation and repeated ordinary-use gates. It does not classify desire versus analysis or infer psychological meaning. Missing or ambiguous evidence permits relevance-only institutionalization or no change.

A durable descendant stores its learned concern and meaning separately from immutable evidence references. Ordinary active-scope Part formation receives these linguistic products, not the supporting audit trajectory. Self receives only ordinary expressed products. Provenance is not an activation cue, autobiographical memory, authority or a task manager. Prediction closure remains independent of an inherited concern's later life. Further structural changes preserve inherited provenance without automatically inventing or transferring a new mission.

Trajectories retain the origin plus recent associated thoughts under a per-investigation bound and disclose omissions. Full history remains researcher-inspectable. Consolidation reads selected expressed products, never raw WorldState or private Part snapshots. Legacy origins can be identified uniquely from recorded creation-cycle cognition and prediction-creation events; ambiguity produces no inferred origin. If a closed investigation lacks a recorded terminal cycle, later source-scope thoughts are conservatively excluded rather than treating the investigation as indefinitely open.

## Verification

Deterministic tests check exact creation linkage, trajectory attribution/bounds, citation rejection, durable persistence and ordinary formation access boundaries. Paired Simulation Tests compare researcher-authored efficiency/desire and analytical/inquiry starting conditions through ordinary experiences, prediction investigation, offline consolidation and transfer probes. They prescribe no activation, prediction classification or learned concern. Mock/mixed observations are plumbing evidence; absent inheritance is distinct from infrastructure failure and configured semantic findings require review.
