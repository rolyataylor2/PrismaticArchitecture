# ADR 0020: Identity Is a Persistent Self-Definition

## Decision

Identity represents the Agent's persistent understanding of who it is and who it wishes to be.

## Reason

Parts provide specialized motivations and consequence forecasting. Identity provides the distinct role through which the complete Agent makes self-directed choices.

## Consequences

Identity may contain values, attributes, roles, aspirations, commitments, and related self-definitions. It is not reduced to a mission-oriented Part.

## Flexible implementation

The permanent representation of Identity remains undecided.
