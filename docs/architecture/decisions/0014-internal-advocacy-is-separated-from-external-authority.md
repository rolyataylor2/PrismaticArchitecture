# ADR 0014: Internal Advocacy Is Separated From External Authority

## Decision
OntoAgentic permits strongly biased and potentially undesirable internal perspectives while preventing individual Parts from independently performing external actions.

## Reason
Internal disagreement helps produce balanced behavior. Forcing every Part to represent globally acceptable behavior would weaken the Parts model.

## Consequences
Parts may internally advocate positions Self rejects while consulting Identity. External behavior remains subject to Self selection, Executive enactment, deployment constraints, and applicable inference-system constraints.

## Flexible implementation
Internal communication mechanisms remain open.
