# ADR 0024: Environments Shape Agents Through Experience

## Decision

External environments influence cognitive development through experiences and consequences rather than direct internal state mutation.

## Reason

OntoAgentic is intended to support growing Agents through interaction rather than scripting final psychological state.

## Consequences

Simulation and external systems communicate events and outcomes. Parts and Identity determine resulting internal development.

## Flexible implementation

Learning algorithms and developmental rates remain open.
