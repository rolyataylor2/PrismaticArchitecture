# ADR 0071: Online Cognition and Offline Consolidation Are Distinct Timescales

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The canonical waking loop interprets an immutable WorldState, activates Parts, supports bids and world-blind Self choice, hands actions to external execution, and later evaluates outcomes. Cross-experience structural reasoning has a different purpose and should not turn every ordinary cycle into global psychological restructuring.

## Decision

Online cognition handles present interpretation, activation, proposals, predictions, choice, action, outcomes, and relatively local/incremental learning. Offline consolidation is a slower developmental process for reasoning across psychologically selected experiences. It may be analogous to sleep or rest but need not correspond to biological sleep. It supplements and does not replace waking cognition.

## Consequences

Consolidation may consider recurrence, abstraction, relational formation, generalization, specialization, split/merge, reconsolidation, association changes, charge development, dormancy/archive, and persistent Identity incongruity. Scheduling, selection algorithms, and thresholds remain open. Identity is not directly rewritten by consolidation; evidence may be surfaced for ordinary mediated development.
