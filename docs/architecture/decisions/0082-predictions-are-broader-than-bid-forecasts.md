# ADR 0082: Predictions Are Broader Than Bid Forecasts

## Decision

Predictions may concern event meaning, unfolding events, social responses, uncertainty resolution, or action consequences. They may precede or exist independently of behavioral bids. Paired selected/non-selected forecasts are not mandatory.

## Consequences

A proposal may refer to an existing prediction or gather information to test it. Prediction formation remains psychologically appropriate rather than schema-driven.
