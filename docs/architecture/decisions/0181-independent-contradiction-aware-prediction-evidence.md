# ADR 0181: Independent, contradiction-aware prediction evidence

Status: Accepted, 2026-10-24. Source: researcher refinement of sleep prediction review concerning self-reinforcing interpretations, evidence recycling and self-fulfilling predictions.

Extends ADR 0180; supersedes its disposition-linked candidate priority. No new disposition class, truth authority, memory trace store or metacognitive controller is introduced. Subjective conviction and biased interpretation remain valid psychological content; evidential certification is distinct from felt confidence.

## Provenance and novelty

Selected external-record citations may accompany ordinary memory encoding. Code verifies each cited source and exact remembered excerpt against the supplied immutable snapshot, preserving stable source/experience identity, original exposure time and source kind. Only selected excerpts enter the existing memory, never an automatic copy of observations or raw archive replay. Recalled-memory and synthesis/reinterpretation ancestry retains exact source versions. Missing legacy provenance remains unknown; runtime does not invent an observation from prose or restore forgotten detail.

Predictions preserve formation/version boundaries and originating source/memory references. Evaluation can occur later than the event, but prospective evidence must have become available after the assessed claim version and cannot already belong to its originating evidence. A later recollection, rewritten memory or synthesized summary does not move its source's exposure time. Revisions preserve history and reset current evaluation/origin boundaries; old validation is not inherited by a revised claim.

Group evidence by underlying experiences rather than memory count, wording or number of retrievals. Memory versions/aliases retain their distinct interpretations but are not independent repetitions. Sleep records newly available versus previously reviewed experience sources. No confidence or establishment increase follows repeated inspection of unchanged sources. Existing conservative institutionalization still requires ended investigations and repeated independently evidenced ordinary usefulness. Weakening or qualifying an interpretation remains possible without forcing neutrality or consensus.

## Contradiction-aware search and outcome testing

Sleep uses the existing independent memory-accessibility scorer in an explicit offline query mode. Queries concern the prediction's conditions/outcomes, with support, contradiction and indeterminate/untested coverage. They receive no disposition concern, activation bias, private Part state or raw WorldState archive. The query-only envelope is not an observation or cognitive cycle; scoring is recorded as offline inspection. Search bounds and omitted candidates are disclosed; absence of selected contrary evidence is not proof none exists. Stratified historical candidates and source diversity prevent linked supportive aliases from occupying the entire budget.

Review separates recorded outcome from interpretation, source stance and whether the predicted conditions were actually tested. It must address contrary and unknown cases. Only a cited observable outcome can certify a prospective test. Finding an object may test recall accuracy without testing an effort-saving claim; the latter needs appropriate comparative cost evidence. A model's interpretation of ambiguity, imagined alternative or repeated self-description cannot supply independent validation.

Review distinguishes observational claims from action-conditioned outcomes and self-influenced or unknown causation. Agent actions and their attributed consequences remain selected experienced records, not privileged execution/audit access. An agent-induced reaction does not independently validate an unconditional claim about others. Preserve causal qualifications rather than converting all observed agreement into generic confirmation.

## Integration and verification

Keep memory/prediction provenance, review/search contracts, provider schemas, editable prompts/descriptions/previews, sleep controls and readable researcher evidence synchronized. Enforce source/version/chronology/novelty gates in code; editable instructions alone are insufficient. Provenance establishes what was supplied, not scientific truth or infallible semantic classification.

Add ordinary-pipeline Simulation Tests with matched truthful/deceptive/ambiguous interactions, repeated reinterpretation controls, claim revision and action-influenced outcomes. Deterministic regressions cover alias/rewrite/replay deduplication, contrary candidate inclusion, old-source rejection, novelty-aware confidence and preserved history. Configured-model behavior remains an empirical question; fixtures cannot establish absence of cognitive bias or human psychological validity.
