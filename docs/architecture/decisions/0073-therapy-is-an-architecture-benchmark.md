# ADR 0073: Therapy Is an Architecture Benchmark

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Ordinary therapeutic interaction is a demanding test of whether persistent biased Parts can change through normal cognition. A product-specific therapy engine or privileged mutation path would bypass the architecture under evaluation.

## Decision

Therapy is both an ordinary external relationship in the psychological process and a benchmark suite for architectural development. Evaluate baseline, interaction, immediate retest, offline consolidation, same-context retest, novel analogous context, and longer-term durability. Measure predictions, memory interpretation, strategies, relationships/conflict, behavior, activation, Identity relation, and appropriate generalization. Conversational compliance alone is not durable change.

## Consequences

No therapy-specific Part mutation authority is introduced. Success does not require lower activation, deletion of protective Parts, merging, or Identity change. Resistance and relationship-specific exceptions remain valid outcomes. Corrective evidence and its later connection to the relevant Part should be inspectable.

## Related decisions

ADR 0064 establishes ordinary cognition for therapeutic change. ADRs 0062 and 0063 define consolidation input and conservatism. ADR 0071 defines the offline developmental timescale.
