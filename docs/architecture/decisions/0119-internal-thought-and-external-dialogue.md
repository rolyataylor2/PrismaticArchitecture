# ADR 0119: Internal thought and external dialogue

## Decision

Thoughts interact with thoughts. Parts may answer, challenge, agree with, or address other Parts through cognition thought text, or through response during Self-mediated deliberation. Such internal exchanges do not require an external bid.

Say and Ask propose speech to external people or entities encountered through WorldState facts. Retained thought history and mediated inquiry responses remain internal subjective contributions even when carried by a WorldState snapshot. They are not external dialogue recipients. An externally directed bid may be informed by internal thoughts, but must not address another Part, Self, a primitive, or a thought as its recipient.

Factory cognition and deliberation prompts distinguish these channels. A Part with only an internal reply contributes thought and no action proposal; during deliberation it withdraws an internally addressed dialogue bid, while an independently justified external bid may remain. Self scores internally addressed Say/Ask bids at zero using only mediated contributions; Self receives no WorldState. Primitive contribution prompts preserve the same dialogue boundary.

## Limits

Recipient meaning is judged by inference, not a name-matching runtime heuristic. No new recipient schema, cognitive access, or inference task is added. Editable overrides remain preserved. External speech realization continues to express selected intentions rather than making a second selection.
