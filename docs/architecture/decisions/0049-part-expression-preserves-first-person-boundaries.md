# ADR 0049: Part Expression Preserves First-Person Boundaries

## Decision

A Part represents itself as `I`, while the complete Agent may be represented as `we`.

## Reason

First-person boundaries preserve differentiated internal perspectives and distinguish a Part from the complete Agent.

## Consequences

Part expression need not use mandatory metacognitive qualifiers. Multiple Part voices must not be flattened before Self receives their bids.

## Flexible implementation

Expression style may vary with Part cognitive sophistication and implementation.
