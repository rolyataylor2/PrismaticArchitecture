# ADR 0137: Experiential disposition development and reconciliation

Status: Accepted, 2026-10-05. Researcher request: implement the conclusions of the [disposition development thread](https://chatgpt.com/share/6ac47fd0-95a8-83e8-86a7-545eed22a83b).

Extends ADRs 0126, 0133, 0134 and 0136. Supersedes offline-only durable disposition formation and unrestricted constructed disposition update proposals. The parallel Part pipeline, immutable snapshot, previous-cycle prerequisite eligibility, next-cycle prediction attention, world-blind Self and external execution remain authoritative. Historical persistent-Part consolidation remains separate.

## Meaning and authority

A disposition is a learned hypothesis that experienced conditions make a family of lived memories relevant. It is not a belief, memory, goal, action, stored Part or world fact. Memories supply lived claims; an instantiated Part forms its current interpretation. Context, internal state and retrieval intent belong to the relevance relationship. Categories are learned from experience rather than chosen from a fixed ontology. Dependencies may form a graph with multiple prerequisites, not a compulsory tree or execution plan.

Pretrained knowledge may interpret supplied experience and inform cognition. It must never count as evidence that the Agent experienced something. Every learned cue requires attributed experiential support. Predictions supply observational bookmarks while open; imagined futures alone cannot establish experiential learning.

Activation, Part cognition, Self and execution do not edit dispositions. Learning runs afterward from committed subjective episodes and resolved prediction evidence. No newly learned disposition participates in the already assembled cycle. Online formation receives the observer's selected experienced details, not omniscient simulation state or another Part's private state. Offline learning uses selected digested evidence; opaque ancestry does not authorize raw audit replay.

## Two developmental speeds

Online formation may return no change, attach evidence to existing sufficient relevance structure, or create a narrow provisional disposition with experience-grounded cues and validated dependencies. It may not specialize, generalize, merge, split, archive or rewrite existing semantic scope. A salient nonprediction episode can warrant provisional formation. Repeated evidence is not a universal creation threshold.

A resolved unexpected outcome can form an ordinary subjective episode immediately. Its prediction/disposition relationship remains pending reconciliation; the event's subjective account is distinct from uncertainty about its structural implications. Successful outcomes and salient unpredicted episodes also contribute evidence. Earlier memories and disposition versions remain preserved. Retrieval can include contradictory episodes, enabling immediate behavioral adaptation before structural consolidation.

Offline reconciliation considers versioned disposition lineage, supporting and contradictory memories, associated predictions, attributed chronological activations and expressed cognitive products. It can preserve, specialize, generalize, split, merge, add/remove dependencies or archive. It must distinguish retrieval usefulness from prediction correctness: relevant memories with a stochastic failed outcome do not by themselves justify structural change. Incomplete, untested or causally incomparable predictions do not establish falsity.

Contrastive proposals identify the smallest experienced difference explaining appropriate versus misleading retrieval, cite episode clusters, preserve alternatives and permit `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`. Generalization requires a shared relevance relationship across meaningfully different experienced contexts. Merge requires repeated same-context activation, substantial recall overlap and shared relevance intent. Archival requires evidence of redundancy or persistent unhelpfulness throughout the claimed scope. Code applies operation-specific conservative evidence gates; models supply meaning, not fabricated statistics. Gates remain configurable experimental policy, not psychological laws.

## Continuity and prospective testing

Structural changes append versions and explicit lineage links. Splits preserve their parent; merges preserve all source lineages; archival preserves evidence and history. Historical activations retain the disposition version actually used. Supporting, contradictory and pending evidence remains inspectable, including originating memory/prediction and cycle/thread references. Statistics are derived from recorded events by code.

Differentiations remain provisional hypotheses. Subsequent ordinary experience can support, qualify, refute or reconcile them back toward preserved parent structure. Confidence is expressed linguistically, not authority or an activation multiplier. Neither promotion nor collapse follows a single bounded outcome classification automatically.

Disposition reconciliation cannot alter or delete underlying memories. Memory reinterpretation remains a distinct explicit versioned operation; changing retrieval scope must not silently change autobiographical content. Existing administrative edits and historical restoration retain their explicit researcher provenance.

## Prompt and research integration

Offline prospective assessment may append an `ASSESS_HYPOTHESIS` operation that changes only linguistic confidence and provisional/established status. It requires repeated recorded retrieval evidence from cycles after the hypothesis was created; establishment additionally requires appropriate judgments. It does not rewrite scope or confer authority. Refuted distinctions can be archived conservatively or refined through existing structural operations while their preserved parents remain available. This makes prospective development explicit rather than an automatic classification-driven promotion.

Online experiential formation and offline structural reconciliation have separate task contracts, provider routes, factory prompts and Settings descriptions. Preview inputs follow runtime access boundaries. Both explain the distinction between relevance and truth, evidence conservation, conservative abstention and pretrained inference versus biography. Runtime validates authority independently of editable prompts.

Simulation Tests must exercise ordinary experiences on isolated Agents with preserved run evidence and controls: meaningful nonprediction formation; immediate failed-outcome memory and waking adaptation; appropriate retrieval despite stochastic failure; contrastive contextual differentiation; generalization across distinct contexts; partial transfer; return to an earlier context without loss of old competence; prospective refinement of provisional hypotheses; and semantic versus descriptive observations for pretrained bootstrapping. Initial conditions are explicit; no fixture forces learned conclusions. Historical primitive participation discussed in the source is superseded by ADR 0133 and is not restored. Mock runs prove plumbing only; configured runs may be inconclusive and do not prove human psychology.

## Implementation and completion evidence

This record defines the requested target, not a claim of runtime completion. Verify:

1. Persistent relevance intent, developmental status, lineage, evidence relationships and historical activation versions, with compatibility for existing records.
2. Separate online formation task invoked after committed memory/outcome products, with evidence-only existing updates and provisional creation; same-cycle activation remains unchanged.
3. Prediction reconciliation provenance and immediate subjective memory availability without destructive older-memory or scope edits.
4. Explicit offline operations, selected lineage/evidence inputs, retrieval-usefulness judgments, abstention, operation-specific validation and atomic preserved-version commits.
5. Independent memory reinterpretation, preserved split/merge/archive history and prospective hypothesis testing through ordinary subsequent experience.
6. Factory prompts, task catalog, routing, preview and researcher inspection synchronized with these contracts.
7. Runnable sourced registry scenarios and meaningful boundary/history regression checks, followed by repository-required validation. Model observations must be reported separately from deterministic verification.
