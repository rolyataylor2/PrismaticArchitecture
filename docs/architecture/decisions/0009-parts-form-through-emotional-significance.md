# ADR 0009: Parts Form Through Emotional Significance

## Decision
Emotional significance is a primary theoretical driver of Part formation and development.

## Reason
Parts represent specialized responses to things that mattered to the Agent. Formation independently of salience would lose an important connection between experience and development.

## Consequences
Formation mechanisms should eventually access some representation of experiential significance. Simulation systems must not assign psychological significance themselves.

## Flexible implementation
No permanent emotion model, threshold, formula, or representation has been selected.
