# ADR 0046: Part Lifecycle Operations Are Semantic

## Decision

Consolidation, splitting, merging, archival, and reactivation are psychologically meaningful operations rather than mechanical database cleanup.

## Reason

Lifecycle operations change the ecology and differentiation of internal perspectives.

## Consequences

Metrics may surface candidates, but semantic evaluation determines whether an operation makes sense. Parent and source ancestry remains recoverable.

## Flexible implementation

Lifecycle state representations and evaluation mechanisms remain open.
